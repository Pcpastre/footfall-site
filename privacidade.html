Footfall Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026.

Footfall is a zombie survival game played by walking around your real city. This page explains what data the game uses, what for, who sees it, for how long and how to delete everything. Contact: contato@footfall.pcpastre.com.

What the game collects

DataWhat forWho sees itHow long
Email and password (the password only in hashed form)Signing in, confirming the email and recovering the passwordNo one but the serverUntil you delete the account
Precise location (GPS), only while a game session is openShowing the map around you, nearby zombies and places, and knowing who is in the same sceneThe server. Other players only see your character when they are in the same scene (within about 70 m). Members of your group see your position on the map while you play.The live position is deleted when you leave the session (at most after 10 minutes standing still). The position shown to your group disappears for others after 3 minutes and is deleted after 1 day.
Nickname and character appearanceShowing you to your group and in the sceneYour group; the appearance, to whoever is in the same sceneUntil you delete the account
Game progress (character, backpack, base, explored map, stamps)Saving the game in the cloudOnly you (the group's base and chests, the group)Until you delete the account
Group messages (may include a marked location)Talking to your groupOnly your groupUntil you delete the account
Market offers and tradesThe player marketOnly you see yours; no one sees what others tradeOffers: until you delete the account. Closed trades are unlinked from you after deletion.
Gravestones (where a character died, no name)Showing gravestones on the mapWhoever passes nearby30 days
App crash reports (error message and version)Fixing crashesOnly the game's developer60 days
A random code for the app installationGiving the first survivor gift only once per deviceNo oneUnlinked from you after deletion

The game does not use your location while the app is closed, does not sell data, does not show ads and does not use your data for advertising. We do not ask for your real name, phone number, photos or contacts.

This website

This website uses no cookies, ads or trackers. When you open the home page, your browser fetches the social and download links from our server (Supabase), without sending any of your data. It only remembers the language you picked, in your own browser. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any server, receives the IP address of visitors.

Where the data is stored

On Supabase (cloud database, São Paulo region, Brazil). The connection between the app and the server is always encrypted. Confirmation and password recovery emails are sent by Resend. The map comes from OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap and the weather from Open-Meteo: for that the app requests the map and weather of the area where you are, without sending your account. The anti-bot check when creating an account, when enabled, is done by Cloudflare Turnstile.

Your rights (Brazil's LGPD)

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or ask any question, by emailing contato@footfall.pcpastre.com. Full deletion can be done inside the game.

Deleting your account

In the game: Menu ⋯ → Settings → Delete account. Without the app: see how to request deletion.

Age

The game is not made for children. You must be 13 or older and, if under 18, have permission from a parent or guardian.

Playing safely

Never play while driving or crossing the street. The game's places are only a reference: do not enter real buildings or private land.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and, if the change is important, we will let you know in the game.

Este site
