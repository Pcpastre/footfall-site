The game

Your city. The dead. You.

Footfall is a gothic pixel-art zombie survival game you play by walking around your real city. The streets, pharmacies, supermarkets and gas stations are the real ones, and they have been overrun.

Walk to find what you need. Loot places that restock every week, treat every wound, raise a base with your group and survive the nights. The more you walk, the further you get.

When you place your first door, a crow lands on it. It stays.

Your city is the map

Real streets and places through GPS. You only see what is in front of you; buildings block your view and night shortens everything.

Real looting

From the door it is quick; going inside there is more stuff, and more zombies. Every piece of furniture makes noise. Everything restocks each week.

Action screen

Fight up close: shove, crouch, aim, throw. Every weapon wears out and makes noise. Anyone within 120 m can join in.

A real body

Wounds treated step by step. Hunger, thirst, fatigue and the real weather. A bite infects, and there is no cure.

Group base

Mark it on the grid, raise walls, add doors, chests, a garden and traps. Every night it is attacked.

Stamp market

A mailbox at your base is everyone's market. The currency is the stamp, and nobody sees what others trade.

Strongholds

Big places have a flag. Beat the guards in the arena and the place belongs to your group, paying stamps every day.

Crow Radio

With a radio you hear about the week's horde, supply drops, clues and missions.

Rare outfits

From common to legendary. Some only drop from the body of a zombie of the same trade: police, firefighter, doctor…

Before you go out

Play with your head up

Watch the trafficNever play while driving. When crossing a street, the game pauses threats: look at the real street.
Places are just a referenceDo not enter buildings, lots or closed areas. Looting happens in the game.
Nobody sees youYour position stays on the server only. Other players never receive where you are.
