Base furniture

All 15 pieces of base furniture: cost, requirement, size, effect and how to place them with the hammer.

Furniture are structures you craft and then place inside the base or in the yard (up to 15 m from the edge). A crafted piece stays "stored" until it is placed: it only works once placed. There are 15 types.

All furniture recipes are of the structure type: they can only be made inside the base (or in the yard, from the action screen). Those marked "workbench" require you to be using the placed workbench. The experience for each is in Recipes.

Crafting, placing, moving and dismantling

  1. Craft: Backpack → CRAFT → Make on the furniture recipe (inside the base). Notice: "... ready. Place it on the action screen (hammer icon, inside the base)."
  2. Open build mode: on the action screen, inside the base, tap the hammer. In build mode, taps don't attack.
  3. Choose: BUILD (door, window, furniture) lists the stored furniture (with quantity) as well as door and window.
  4. Place: the ghost furniture appears in front of the figure, snapped to a 0.5 m grid aligned with the base. Walk and aim to choose the spot, rotate 90° with the rotate button and tap the check mark. Outside the base and yard the check mark is grayed out ("Outside the base and the yard (15 m from the edge).").
  5. Use: outside build mode, get within 1.3 m of the furniture's edge and tap USE ... (the label changes with the furniture).

In build mode, near a placed piece of furniture you get:

  • Move: picks up the furniture as a ghost again (keeps the rotation);
  • Store: removes the furniture from the ground without dismantling it ("... stored (hammer to place it again).");
  • Dismantle: returns all the material to the backpack. The chest must be empty ("Empty the chest first.") and the garden bed must have no plant ("Harvest (or wait) before dismantling the garden bed."). Building a dismantled piece again gives no experience.

Two pieces of furniture can't be too close together ("Too close to another piece of furniture."). Stored furniture doesn't work: for example, a stored chest doesn't receive anything.

Slipping toolsEvery Carpentry or Mechanics recipe has a chance of cutting your hand: 3% minus 0.5% per skill level (gone at level 6). The cut is a real wound on the hand.

Workbench

Unlocks the recipes marked "workbench" in Recipes. A base can only have one workbench ("The base already has a workbench."). Use it with USE WORKBENCH: it opens the CRAFT tab, which uses what's in the backpack and in the chests. When you're not using the workbench, workbench recipes warn "Walk to the workbench on the action screen and tap USE.". There are also workbenches in the radio vault and in the basements of the bunkers, but they don't make furniture.

Chest

Each placed chest holds 40 kg. USE CHEST opens that chest; the Base → CHESTS tab shows all chests together (inside the base, it stores into the first chest with space). The chests count as stock for recipes, repairs, reinforcements and the market while you're at the base. What's in the chests can vanish in the night attack if the base is open. To dismantle one, empty it first.

Bed

When you end the session inside the base (by GPS) with a bed placed, rest with the app closed goes 50% further (rest rate 18.75 versus 12.5) and health comes back at 7.5 per hour instead of 5 (with no bleeding or infection). USE BED just reminds you: "End the session inside the base to sleep: with a bed, rest goes 50% further." Entering the base through an outpost and ending at the outpost doesn't use the bed.

Table

USE TABLE opens the backpack with the message "Eating at the table: food goes 10% further.". While that screen is open, any food eaten gives 10% more calories (spoiled food doesn't get the bonus).

Garden bed

Each garden bed is a plot of the garden. At most 4 per base ("The garden already has 4 garden beds."). USE GARDEN BED shows the status and the Plant, Water and Harvest buttons. On the action screen the drawing changes: empty, sprouting or ripe.

Rain barrel

With the app open and rain falling, each placed barrel collects 1 bottle every 10 minutes, up to 20. (Gaps of more than 30 min without updating don't count.) The button shows BARREL WATER (N): tapping it fills as many empty bottles in the backpack as possible with rainwater, which needs to be boiled or filtered. See Cooking.

Campfire and wood stove

Both are for cooking. LIGHT CAMPFIRE / LIGHT WOOD STOVE uses 1 plank (with a lighter, which isn't used up) and keeps the fire lit for 30 minutes; once lit, the button becomes COOK. Difference:

Stretcher (infirmary)

With a stretcher placed and you at the base by GPS, the healing time of treated wounds drops to 67% (they heal 50% faster). USE STRETCHER (INFIRMARY) opens the Health screen.

Stakes, tin can wire and covered pit

These are the traps: stakes wound and cripple (6 uses), the pit traps (3 uses) and the cans make noise when a zombie passes (they don't wear out). Tapping USE STAKES or USE COVERED PIT re-arms them. In the night attack, the cans reduce damage.

Floodlight

The floodlight only turns on with a portable generator stored in one of the base chests. USE FLOODLIGHT refuels it: 1 gas can = 24 h of tank (added to what was already there). It only turns on at night. When on:

  • the base appears lit on the action screen at night;
  • in the night attack, it draws 2 more zombies, but each blow does 20% less (you can see who's coming).

Watchtower

With a tower placed, while you're less than 40 m from the base, your vision range on the map is 50% larger. In the night attack it also reduces damage by 15%.

Mailbox

Gives access to the market and the warehouse from inside the base (USE MAILBOX). Without it, the only post office is the one in the radio vault.
