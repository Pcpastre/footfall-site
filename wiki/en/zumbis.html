Zombies

How zombies live on the map (dormant, wandering and downed), how they notice and chase you, where they spawn, when they come back and every uniform type with its multipliers.

The city has a fixed population of zombies: each stretch of street has its own, always the same ones during the month, and every phone sees the same zombies in the same place. Some died in uniform (police officers, doctors, soldiers...) and are tougher and carry job-specific loot.

The three states on the map

StateChanceBehavior
Dormant65%standing still, eyes half closed. Sees and hears at half strength until it notices something. At night, 3 in every 8 get up and roam (6 in 8 on a full moon)
Wandering25%walks a little along the streets near home and stops for a while (by day it rests 20 to 60 s, at night 8 to 25 s, in a storm half that); each walk lasts 5 to 15 s
Downed10%lying down like a corpse. It doesn't see; it only hears up close (30% of the radius). It only gets up when it hears something or when a fight starts near it

Zombies move along the streets (the map's street graph). Near you, when chasing, they leave the street and come straight at you (within 20 m, or when they're on the same stretch of street). When roaming, none of them strays more than 40 m from the spot where it lives; if it does, it comes back.

In the small hours they all move 15% faster. On the map, speed doesn't change with the uniform; only on the action screen.

How they notice you

  • Sight: a 100° cone in the direction it's looking, 15 m by day, 8 m at night, 30 m if your flashlight is on. It notices you gradually: 1 s up close, 2 s at the edge. Details in Vision.
  • Hearing: radius = noise × 120 m. Within 8 m it comes right away; within 100 m it goes to the sound. Details in Noise.
  • Neighbors: one that has seen you makes all others within 20 m of it start chasing too.
  • Only zombies within 150 m of you "think" (move and notice). Those more than 750 m away leave memory and remember where they were.

Chasing and giving up

  1. When chasing, it takes the shortest street path to you.
  2. If it gets within 25 m, the action screen starts.
  3. If it goes 15 s without seeing or hearing you (and is more than 20 m away), it loses the trail and goes to the last place it saw you.
  4. There, it searches for 8 s, looking around. Then dormant and downed ones go back to sleep; wandering ones go back to roaming.
  5. More than 450 m from you it gives up immediately.
  6. Inside a closed base, they all give up and forget the sound.

A zombie that was drawn somewhere else (by an alarm, a sound or a chase) starts living where it ended up.

Where they spawn and how many

The city is divided into cells of about 100 × 100 m. Each cell has:

CellZombies
With loot places10 + 4 per place "guard" + 0 to 4, between 5 and 30. Each place counts by its type's number of guards (Health, Market and Police 3; Restaurant, Construction, Outpost, Fire station and School 2; the others 1)
No places, in a green area5
No places, ordinary street6 to 10

Within the cell, street points within 40 m of a loot place are 4× more likely to get a zombie. No one spawns inside your base. The population is generated within 500 m of you.

Inside places there are other zombies, which depend on the place's type and size; those come back every week (see Looting).

When they come back

  • Once killed on the map, a zombie doesn't come back until the month turns over.
  • In the new month they all come back ("New month: the zombies are back."), with the same distribution for everyone.
  • Zombies inside places come back every week; bunker zombies come back on every visit.

Where each one appears

Each zombie's type is rolled deterministically (every phone sees the same clothes):

  • Near a job's place (a place within 50 m of where it lives): a 25% chance of being one of that place's types.
  • On the street: otherwise, a 7% chance of being special, with these weights: backpacker 20%, soldier 18%, police officer 12%, doctor 12%, construction worker 10%, inmate 8%, security guard 8%, firefighter 6%, supermarket clerk 6%.
  • The rest are common zombies.

Zombies inside a place follow the same rule with the place's own type. Other sources:

  • Weekly horde: 6% soldiers (the ones who tried to contain it), plus 7% street specials.
  • Bunker: 50% + 12% per floor are soldiers (85% at most), plus the officer and the Brute. See Bunkers.
  • Rescue flare: easy 8% police officers; medium 15% inmates and 15% police officers; hard 25% soldiers, 15% firefighters and 10% inmates, and the Brute at the head of the last wave. See Horde and alarms.

Loot and uniform

Besides the common pocket, special zombies carry job-specific loot (each page has the table) and a 10% chance of the uniform's outfit (the Brute has none). See Bodies.

Attacks and wounds

On the action screen zombies scratch (4 to 7), grab (40% of the time, at most 2 at once) and bite (6 to 10). Crawlers bite feet and legs (3 to 5). All with a visible wind-up beforehand. Details in Melee combat.

The Brute

The Brute is the boss: 6 times a zombie's health, a doubled knockdown blow and a straight-line charge. It appears at the head of the last wave of the hard flare and in the vault antechamber of bunkers.
