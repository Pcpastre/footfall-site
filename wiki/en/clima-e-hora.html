Weather and time

The real weather and real time of the place where you play, and everything that rain, fog, storms, wind, temperature, night, the small hours and the full moon change.

Footfall uses the real weather and the real time where you are. If it's raining on your street, it rains in the game; if it's gotten dark, it's night in the game. This page lists every effect.

Where the weather comes from

  • The game queries the free Open-Meteo service, sending only your position rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km).
  • It updates when the session starts and then every 30 min. Without internet, the last reading stays in effect.
  • It reads: precipitation, weather code, temperature at 2 m, wind speed and direction at 10 m.

Fog

  • Your vision range on the map: ×0.6.
  • Vision range on the action screen: ×0.6 (notice "Fog: see up to X m").
  • It doesn't change the zombies' vision.

Storm

When it starts, you see "Storm: the thunder muffles your footsteps, but they get restless."

Wind

  • Wind above 30 km/h makes a pitched tent's tarp wear out 1.5× faster (if there's no rain or storm, which already count).
  • The rest is ambience: wind sound (from 12 km/h) and the direction of things blowing on the map and on the action screen.

Temperature

The feels-like temperature is the real temperature minus 4 °C in the rain (without a raincoat or windbreaker) and minus 2 °C at night.

Cold

Cold only counts with the session open and outside the base. The warm clothing you need:

Feels likeWarm clothing points needed
16 °C or more0
from 10 °C to under 16 °C1
below 10 °C2

Each piece worn and intact adds up: leather jacket 1, sweatshirt 1, jacket with pockets 1, beanie 1, thick coat 2. Torn clothing doesn't keep you warm. Whatever is missing becomes the cold level (0 to 2: "Cold", "Freezing"). Eating hot soup removes the cold for a while.

Heat

Heat only counts during the day, with the session open. Heavy pieces: the coat counts 2; the leather jacket, ballistic vest, tactical vest, ballistic helmet, jacket with pockets, tactical helmet, firefighter helmet and expedition pack count 1.

Real temperatureNo heavy pieceWith 1 pointWith 2 or more
up to 28 °C000

A cap worn removes 1 heat level.

What cold and heat do

  • Cold: burns 0.004 kcal per second per level (shivering).
  • Heat: uses 0.012 water per second per level (sweating).
  • Each level of cold or heat takes 8 points off max stamina.

Night

Night runs from sunset to sunrise at your location, calculated from your position and the date. When it gets dark: "Night has fallen: zombies are more active. The flashlight draws attention from afar."

Every night, at 1 a.m., the base suffers a night attack (with the app closed, up to 3 missed nights are resolved when you come back). The mail crow doesn't fly at night: it lands and finishes the flight in the morning.

Small hours

This is the part of the night before 5 a.m. Notice: "Small hours: the zombies are restless and hear better."

  • The radius at which zombies hear you becomes ×1.3.
  • Map zombies move 15% faster (chasing, investigating or wandering).

Full moon

The game calculates the real moon phase. It counts as a full moon when the phase is within 0.04 of the middle of the cycle (a day or so on each side). It only applies at night. Notice: "Full moon: you can see farther, but so can they. More of them roam tonight."

EffectValue
Your vision range without a flashlight40 × 1.5 = 60 m
Range at which zombies see you without a flashlight8 × 1.3 = 10.4 m
Sleepers that get up and wander6 out of every 8 (instead of 3)

Game time

Online, the game clock follows the server time. Setting your phone's clock forward or back doesn't change the night, the week, the month, place stock or events.
