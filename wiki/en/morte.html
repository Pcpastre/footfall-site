Death

What happens when your survivor dies: the pile with everything you were carrying, the zombie with your face, the wallet, what stays, the gravestones and the new character.

Death in Footfall is permanent for the character: profession, traits and skills are gone. But the world goes on: the base, the chests and most of the wallet stay, and everything you were carrying stays on the ground where you fell, with zombie-you on top of it, waiting for the next survivor to come get it.

How you die

CauseMessage
Health reached 0 on the map with a wound bleedingYou bled to death.
Health reached 0 on the map with no bleedingYou succumbed to hunger and thirst.
Health reached 0 on the action screenKilled by the horde.
End of the 7-day deadline of the zombie infection, while playingThe infection won.
End of the infection deadline with the app closedThe infection won while you were away.

On the map, health drops from bleeding, a forgotten tourniquet, a wound infected for more than 72 h, and hunger and thirst below 10%. On the action screen, from the hits of zombies and guards.

The death screen

When you die, the session ends and the This is how you died screen appears, with the crow and the line "Nevermore.". It shows the cause, the profession, how long you survived and three numbers from your life: Distance (km), Zombies killed and Looted places. The buttons are New survivor (goes straight to profession and traits) and View map. The summary of your last character also stays on the panel's Stats tab, under THE LAST ONE.

The death pile

Everything you were carrying becomes a pile where you fell: the whole backpack, what was in your pockets, the clothes you were wearing, what was on your belt and back, and the weapon in your hand.

PeriodWho sees it and can take from it
First 24 hOnly you (your next character). On the map it shows as Your body, and the container is called What you were carrying.
From 24 h to 4 daysAny survivor who passes nearby. To others it shows as Survivor body, with the Dead man's backpack.
After 4 daysThe pile disappears.
  • The pile also disappears when it's empty.
  • With an account and internet, the 24 h and 4 days count from the moment the pile reaches the server. If you died without a connection, the pile is kept on the phone and goes up the next time there's a network; until then, only you see it.
  • Looting the pile is the same as searching a body. When two people take the same item, the server confirms it and whoever took it first gets it.

The zombie with your face

On top of each pile stands a zombie with your appearance: the dead character's skin and clothing colors, faded. It appears when the pile is visible to you and you're within 500 m of it, and it starts asleep (it sees and hears at half strength). It stays there until someone takes it down; once taken down, it doesn't come back, neither for you nor for other players. Remember that it's you: to get your things back, you first have to bring down your old character.

Get back what you lostYou have 24 exclusive hours. The new character starts with the basic kit (a bat and, if it's been more than 24 h since the last one, a revolver), enough to face a sleeping zombie. Take it slow, by day if you can.

The wallet

The stamps in the wallet stay with the account, minus 15% (rounded), which vanish from the game. E.g.: with 40 stamps you lose 6 and keep 34. The loss shows in the log as "Lost N stamp(s) from the wallet.".

What you lose and what stays

LostStays
Profession and traitsThe base, the walls and the furniture
All skills and XPThe chests and the base's stock
Everything you were carrying (goes to the pile)85% of the wallet's stamps
15% of the stampsThe outfits in the wardrobe and the memories on the bookshelf
Wounds, infections and active medicationYour appearance and nickname
Getting used to panic (days alive)The clock for the kit's revolver

Hunger, thirst, tiredness and health go back to normal for the new character, and the mind (stress, sadness, boredom) is reset at creation. If you died inside a bunker, the next character doesn't inherit the safe room.

Gravestones and markers

  • On the map (with an account): each death becomes a gravestone on the spot. Other survivors' gravestones nearby appear without a name, only with how many days the person survived (N day(s)); yours appear as You fell here. The list of nearby gravestones updates every 5 minutes or when you walk 400 m.
  • On the explored map: the places where you died are marked with a red X.
  • In the group: the Memorial (social tab, "Who in the group is down") lists the members who died, with nickname, days, kills, km and cause. When empty, it says "No one yet. Let's keep it that way.".

The new survivor

After death, create another character with the New survivor button on the death screen or with Create character on the map's "Your survivor died" card. The appearance step is skipped (it stays the same) and you go straight to profession and traits. They get the basic kit again, but:

  • the .38 revolver and its ammo only come if 24 h have passed since the last kit with a revolver;
  • the first character bonus (outpost radio and 10 stamps) isn't repeated.
