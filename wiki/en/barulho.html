Noise

Every noise source in the game, with values, and the math behind the radius in which zombies hear you.

Zombies hear as well as they see. On the map, everything you do has a noise level from 0 (silence) to 1 (maximum), and that level becomes a radius in meters: anyone inside the radius hears you. On the action screen noise is measured directly in meters. This page lists every source.

The radius math on the map

Hearing radius = noise × 120 m (120 m is the noise of a car). Then:

FactorEffect on the radius
Rain×0.7
Storm (the thunder muffles footsteps)×0.6

On the zombie's side, the radius changes further:

ZombieRadius it uses
Awake (wandering, or dormant that has already woken up)the full radius
Dormant that hasn't noticed anything yethalf
Downed, lying down30%
Any of them, in the small hours (night before 5 AM)×1.3

What happens when it hears you:

  • within 8 m: it comes after you immediately;
  • within 100 m (or if it was already heading toward another sound): it wakes up and goes to the spot where it heard you. Once there (within 8 m), it searches for 8 s and, if it is dormant or downed, goes back to "sleep".

The noise level doesn't change all at once: every half second it moves 60% of the way when rising and only 15% when falling. Running for a bit leaves a noise "trail" for a few seconds after you stop. The meter at the top of the map shows the current radius.

Looting on the map

With a loot screen open, the noise is at least 0.02 (2.4 m). While you rummage through a piece of furniture, it rises to that furniture's noise, from 0.02 to 0.25 (2.4 to 30 m). The Clumsy trait multiplies furniture noise by 1.5. The full furniture table is in Looting. The game sums up each piece of furniture as:

  • silent: below 0.10 (less than 12 m);
  • moderate noise: from 0.10 to 0.17 (12 to 20 m);
  • loud noise: 0.18 or more (21.6 m or more).

On the action screen, finishing rummaging through a piece of furniture makes a noise of furniture noise × 120 m around it.

Gunshots

Inside the action screen

WeaponShot radius in the scene
Firearms in generalthe whole scene (every unaware zombie wakes up)
.22 rifle320 m (in practice, the whole scene: the arena is 120 m)
9 mm with a silencer30 m
Bow and crossbow5 m (and an arrow that hits makes no noise)

A wall in between halves the reach of any noise in the scene: anyone behind a wall only wakes up if they're in the nearer half of the radius.

After the fight, on the map

When the fight ends, the gunshots echo across the map:

  • any loud shot (no silencer, not .22, including shotgun blasts): every zombie on the map within 150 m of the fight's location that wasn't chasing you heads there;
  • only .22 shots: the same, but within 52.5 m (35% of 150);
  • silenced shots and arrows don't count.

See Firearms.

Traits that change what you hear

  • Keen hearing: the warning of a zombie approaching out of sight (a groan from the side it's coming from) reaches 70 m instead of 40 m. On the action screen, the sound markers around your character reach 10 m instead of 6 m.
  • Bad hearing: no groan warning on the map; on the action screen, sound markers only at 3 m.
  • Full-face helmet (motorcycle, riot): the action screen's sound markers are 30% smaller.
