Infection

The zombie infection (stages, 7-day deadline, chance per wound and per trait) and the common wound infection, which antibiotics cure.

Footfall has two kinds of infection, which have nothing to do with each other:

  • Zombie infection: comes from wounds caused by zombies. It has no cure and kills in 7 days of real time.
  • Common infection: sets in on a dirty or poorly treated wound. It gets in the way a lot, but an Antibiotic cures it.

Zombie infection

How you catch it

Every wound a zombie causes rolls, on the spot, whether it passes on the infection:

WoundNormalStrong immunityWeak immunity
Bite85%60%100%
Laceration25%12.5%40%
Hemorrhage25%12.5%40%
Scratch7%3.5%11.2%
Any other (bruise, fracture, burn, cut, shard)0%0%0%

When clothing stops a bite, it becomes a bruise and doesn't infect. Wounds from guards and from accidents (broken glass, tools, fire) never pass on the zombie infection. Being bitten always shows "You were bitten..." and adds 20 stress and 15 sadness, whether it infects or not.

There is only one infection: if you are already infected, another infected wound doesn't change the clock. The deadline counts from the moment of the first wound that infected you.

Stages

Time since the woundStageMoodleEffects
0 to 3 daysIncubating—No symptoms ("Bitten. No symptoms yet.")
3 to 5 daysFeverFeverish (level 2)−25 max stamina; −10% combat damage; stress rises
5 to 7 daysDeliriumDelirious (level 3)−35 max stamina; map vision ×0.6; −20% combat damage; stress rises
7 daysDeath—"The infection won."

The days are real and keep running with the app closed. If the deadline runs out while you're away, the character dies when you open the game ("The infection won while you were away."). See Death.

In the Health tab, while there is an infection, the stage text appears along with "Time left: ... (real time). There's no cure yet.".

Other effects

  • While infected, you don't recover health by sleeping (not at the base, not in a bed, not in a sleeping bag). While playing, health keeps coming back at 1 per minute if the other conditions allow it (see Needs).
  • During fever and delirium, stress rises 0.05 per second with the app open (see Mind).
  • No item or medicine cures or delays the zombie infection. Antibiotics have no effect on it.

How to avoid it

  • Wear protection on your arms and torso, where most bites land (see Wounds). Leather and rigid pieces stop 85% to 95% of bites where they cover.
  • The Strong immunity trait lowers bites to 60% and cuts the chance from lacerations and scratches in half.
  • A standing zombie only bites after grabbing you (or when you are down on the ground); a crawler bites right away. Break free of a grab quickly and avoid being knocked down.
Bitten isn't dead (yet)There's a 15% chance a bite doesn't infect. And even infected you have 7 real days: time to take what matters to the base and the chests, which remain after death.

Common infection

How you catch it

For every hour with an active session, each wound that can bleed (scratch, laceration, bite, deep cut, shard; not hemorrhage) has a chance that is the sum of:

SituationChance per hour
Bandaged without being cleaned6%
Dirty dressing (a dressing older than 12 h, 24 h with medical tape, or a rag one)4%
Shard still inside5%
No dressing at all (except scratches)2%

A burn without ointment has 3% per hour, separately. Bruises and fractures never get infected. Example: a laceration bandaged with a rag and not cleaned adds up to 6% + 4% = 10% per hour.

The chance is only rolled with the app open, outside the action screen. With the app closed, the wound doesn't get infected.

Effects

  • The wound becomes INFECTED and stops healing (even with every step done).
  • Pain +10 on that wound.
  • The Infected wound moodle (level 2) and −15 max stamina.
  • With the app open, health doesn't come back.
  • After 72 h infected, the wound takes 1 HP every 600 s (6 per hour).

Cure

Take an Antibiotic (Take in the inventory): it cures the common infection on all wounds at once ("Antibiotic: the wound infection will go away.") and also restores 20 health (plus 10% per First aid level). The wound starts healing again from zero. Taking it without an infection uses up the medicine just the same ("Took an antibiotic (there was no infected wound).").

How to avoid it

  • Clean before bandaging (Saline, Alcohol or Cachaça), or bandage with a Sterile dressing or a First aid kit, which already count as cleaning.
  • Remove the shard right away.
  • Change the dressing when dirty dressing appears, and use Medical tape to make it last 24 h.
  • Avoid bandaging with a rag, unless you're in a pinch.
