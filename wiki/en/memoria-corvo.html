The crow

The last memory: no fighting, you fly as the crow while Poe's poem appears stanza by stanza.

The crow is the seventh and last memory. There's no fighting: you are the crow and you fly, while the poem "The Raven", by Edgar Allan Poe, appears stanza by stanza.

Sheet
ProtagonistThe crow ("He saw it all")
Where to findAppears on its own on the shelf once the other six memories have been finished. There is also the book Memory: The raven (an old book of poems, with a black feather marking "The Raven")
Challenge typeNone: "Just fly. And read."
Starting kitNone
RewardTrophy: your crow gets a ribbon from each memory on its leg, which shows on its drawing on the map. It doesn't give an outfit set.

How to unlock

Finish The last delivery, The night shift, Old Jorge, The crow's voice, Thursday duty and The vault. Skipping or leaving midway doesn't count: each one must have been finished. Before that, the sheet says "Appears once the other six have been played."

How to play

  • Dragging on the screen tilts the flight (up and down). The path is always the same.
  • Tapping advances the stanza. There are 3 stanzas per scene.
  • The poem appears in the game's language when there's a translation; without one, in the English original (public domain).
  • Like the others, playing ends the session.
