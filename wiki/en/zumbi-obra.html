Zombie: Construction worker

Construction worker in a reflective vest and hard hat.

Construction worker in a reflective vest and hard hat. The hard hat removes 40% of head damage. He carries building materials and tools.

Shotgun fire ignores the uniform's protection. The full multipliers are in Melee combat and Firearms.

Behavior

On the map he's like any zombie: he can be dormant, wandering or fallen, sees in a 100° cone and hears your noise (see Zombies). The uniform only changes combat and loot.

Where he appears

  • Near and inside construction stores: 25% of zombies within 50 m.
  • On the street: 10% of street specials.

Loot

Every body has the regular pocket: 45% chance of cloth rags, 0 to 2 things from the pocket list and a 6% chance of a stamp (see Bodies).

On top of that, the trade loot. Each row is rolled separately; the quantity, when there is one, goes from 1 up to the maximum, with the same chance for each number.

Tips
