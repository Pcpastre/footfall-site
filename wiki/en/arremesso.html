Throwing

Everything you can throw on the map and on the action screen, with range, effect and duration, plus the Molotov cocktail's fire.

Throwing things is for distraction: the noise pulls zombies away from you, and on the action screen the firework stuns and the Molotov burns. There are five throwable items.

The Molotov is made without a workbench: 3 bottles, 1 gas can and 3 rags make 3 Molotovs.

On the map

  • The ball button appears when you have an alarm clock, firework, bottle or rock. With more than one type, it opens the list so you can choose.
  • The item lands 18 m + 1.5 m per Strength level in front of you (where your character is facing). If there is a wall before that, it lands 1 m short of it; never less than 3 m away.
  • Every zombie within the item's radius that wasn't chasing you wakes up, forgets what it was doing and goes to the landing spot. There it searches for 8 s.
  • The landing spot is marked on the map.
  • Each throw gives 3 Stealth experience.

Messages: "The firework went off N m away. The whole neighborhood will come see what it was.", "The alarm clock is ringing N m away. They'll go there.", "Bottle smashed N m away. Anyone nearby will see.", "Rock landed N m away."

On the action screen

  • You can only throw what is in your pockets. Anything at the bottom of the backpack has to be dug out first (2.5 s; see Backpack and weight).
  • The button sits next to CROUCH, with the name (MOLOTOV, FIREWORK, WATCH, BOTTLE, ROCK) and how many are left. Tap to throw; hold to switch to the next type.
  • If you haven't picked one, the button starts in this order: Molotov, firework, alarm clock, bottle, rock.
  • The item lands 9 m in front of you (14 m at most). If there is a wall in the way, it lands before it.
  • You can't throw at the ground.

How zombies react to a distraction

  • An unaware zombie within the radius goes to the spot of the noise.
  • An alert zombie within the radius loses you and goes to the noise if: it is more than 6 m from you, it isn't grabbing you or in the middle of an attack, and it doesn't have you in sight with a clear line.

Firework

It goes off where it lands ("BOOM!"). Anyone within 3 m is thrown away from the blast (2.5 m/s for 0.6 s) and has a 50% chance of falling. Afterwards it draws anyone within 60 m.

Alarm clock

It keeps ringing for 30 s ("brrring"). Every 2 s, the ringing draws the zombies within 35 m again. Good for holding a group away while you rummage.
