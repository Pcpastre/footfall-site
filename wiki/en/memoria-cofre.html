The vault

The soldier's memory in the hilltop bunker: defending against the horde, falling back in stages and the vault door.

The vault is the memory of a soldier in a military bunker, one month after it all began. It is the longest combat memory: defending against a horde, position by position.

Fact sheet
ProtagonistPrivate Nunes, soldier · day 30
Where to find itThe book Memory: The vault (the bunker's guard log, with the name Nunes), inside military bunkers, with a 0.6% chance per bunker
Challenge typeDefending against the horde, falling back and the vault door: several positions, each with a time to hold out (the clock is at the top of the screen)
WeatherNight, no rain
ProfessionMilitary veteran (Firearms 2, Melee weapons 1 and the Desensitized trait, free with the profession), no other traits
Starting kit1 5.56 rifle (already in hand), 210 5.56 ammo, 1 Combat knife, 2 Bandage; wearing the Daypack
RewardThe Soldier uniform (camouflage uniform, helmet and tactical vest), with the name Nunes embroidered on the chest
ScoreKills

Gameplay tips

  • Each position's clock only runs while you are in it. Hold out for the time and fall back when the game tells you to.
  • 210 rounds seems like a lot, but the horde is big: fire in short bursts and use the ammo points when they show up.
  • Keep ammo in your pockets to reload quickly; what's in the backpack takes longer.
  • Floodlights help you see who's coming, but they draw attention.
  • Save the bandages for bleeding: bleeding in the middle of the defense is what takes people down most.
  • If you go down, FROM HERE restarts from the last checkpoint.
