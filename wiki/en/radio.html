Wiki / Crow and radio

Radio

How to listen to Crow Radio and everything it gives you: the weekly horde, supply drops, the phrase of the day, the clue of the day, the weekly mission and alarms.

Crow Radio is the world's news source. It warns you about the weekly horde and supply drops, gives one clue to untouched stock per day and the mission of the week. The panel is in the menu (...) → Radio, which shows how many new messages there are (Radio (2 new messages)). Without a radio: "Needs a radio".

How to get a radio

You hear the radio if you have any one of these:

  • a Radio in your backpack;
  • a Radio in a base chest;
  • a Radio at the radio depot;
  • an outpost set up with an Outpost radio (the free outpost doesn't count).

Where to find a Radio: the police, fire station, electronics, supply drop and bunker lists, and the body of the security guard zombie (15%). Without a radio, the panel says: "Without a radio you hear nothing. Find a radio (police, fire station, electronics) or set up an outpost with the outpost radio."

With a radio, the station is checked every 20 seconds during the session. New messages appear as a notice on screen ("Radio: ...") and stay on the panel (the last 30), with the weekday and time.

Weekly horde

Every week, each region of about 2 km has one horde that crosses the region in a straight line. Everyone who lives nearby sees the same horde.

HordeValue
Size30 to 60 zombies
DayWednesday to Sunday
Start timebetween 7 PM and 10:45 PM (in 15-minute steps)
Duration2 hours
Route3 km in a straight line, passing within 500 m of the region's center
Radio warningfrom Monday (6 days before) until the end of the crossing

The message gives the approximate size, where it's coming from, the day and the time and, if you have a base, whether the route passes near it: "Horde of about 45 coming from the southwest: Friday at 20:30. Passes about 150 m from your base." or "... The route is far from your base.". When it starts moving: "The horde is moving now. Stay away from its route or defend the base."

The radio panel shows a red box with the horde while it's announced. On the map, the pack's position appears for anyone with a radio or within 400 m of it. The pack's zombies become real zombies when the pack passes within 500 m of you. If the route passes within 300 m of the base, it hits the walls: see Night attack.

Supply drops

Two crates per week per region, dropped by helicopter:

CrateValue
How many2 per week: one between Monday and Wednesday, another between Thursday and Saturday
Timebetween 10 AM and 6:59 PM
Where200 m to 1.1 km from the region's center (snapped to the nearest street)
Stays on the ground24 hours, or until someone empties it
Landing noise20 minutes: zombies within 300 m head to it
Contents5 to 7 rolls from the supplies list; ammo comes with 4 to 9 extra; stamps come with 2 to 5 extra. The same for everyone.

With a radio, you always know: "Radio: a helicopter dropped a supply drop 650 m to the north." Without a radio, you only hear it if you're within 1.5 km: "A helicopter flew low and dropped something to the north." On the map, the crate appears for anyone with a radio, anyone who heard it land and anyone within 200 m.

Phrase of the day

Every day the radio says one phrase, the same for everyone that day (it goes onto the panel without an on-screen notice):

  • "This is Crow Radio. If you can hear this, someone's still alive on this side."
  • "Boil the water. Always. The rain also brings what they left on the roof."
  • "They don't sleep. They just stop. Any noise and the whole block wakes up."
  • "Don't open the door to whoever knocks three times. Living people knock and call out your name."
  • "The north side hospital fell on the third night. No one came back from there with a full backpack."
  • "If you have a radio, listen at night. That's when the signal crosses the city."
  • "The crows are back in the square. They always know where there's food."
  • "Walk slowly. Running draws attention, and stamina is the one thing you can't find in a store."
  • "If the horde passes down your street, put out the campfire and stay quiet until dawn."
  • "The helicopter crate is no gift. Everyone heard it fall."
  • "It's been a month today. Some still plant on the rooftops. Some still wait."
  • "Nevermore. That's what the crow says. Don't believe him."

After you finish the memory The crow's voice, the radio panel gets a special line at the top.

Clue of the day

Once a day, the radio points to a place not yet looted between 300 m and 1.2 km from you: "Weak signal: there's untouched stock at (place), about 800 m to the east." The first time you rummage through that place, it has one extra rare item (from the lists for that type of place; if the list has no rare item, a box of canned food). The clue is rolled per player.

Mission of the week

Every Monday (the first time the radio is heard that week), a short mission comes out. In a group, the mission is the same for everyone.

MissionGoalWhat counts
Kill zombies25The character's kills this week
Loot places6Places looted this week
Walk in session8 kmKilometers walked in session this week
Gather planks12Extra planks in the base chests compared to the start of the week

Reward: 10 stamps, delivered automatically when the bar fills ("Mission accomplished. We left you 10 stamps. Good work."). Only what you gathered since the announcement counts. The panel shows the mission, the progress bar and "Pays 10 stamps." / "Completed: 10 stamps delivered.". You need a radio to receive the mission and for it to be checked.

Full moon and storm

The panel tells you when they're in effect:

  • Full moon (full moon night): "Full moon: more of them roaming; you and they see farther." At night, your vision on the map reaches 50% farther.
  • Storm: "Storm: the thunder muffles your footsteps; they get restless." Your vision drops 15%.

Alarms

Regardless of the radio, every now and then an alarm goes off somewhere near you during the session:

AlarmValue
Interval8 to 15 minutes between one alarm and the next
WhereA place on the map between 300 and 600 m from you
Duration3 minutes
EffectZombies within 300 m of it that aren't chasing you head there; when it ends, they start "living" nearby

Notice: "An alarm went off: (place). Zombies in the area are heading there." A supply drop landing works like a 20-minute alarm. See Horde and alarms.
