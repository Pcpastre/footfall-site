Walls, doors and windows

Costs, health, reinforcement and repair of each wall, door and window of the base.

The walls of the base are the sides of the polygon you marked out. Each real wall has its own health and a reinforcement level (wood, sheet metal or concrete). Doors and windows cut into a wall and are the weak point in the night attack.

For reinforcement, N = ⌈length ÷ 3 m⌉ (minimum 1): a 7 m wall needs 3 sheet metal and 6 nails to become sheet metal, and then 3 cement and 3 water bottles to become concrete.

On the action screen reinforced walls show the label "sheet metal" or "concrete". The panel shows how many walls you have of each type (e.g.: "Walls: 3 wood, 1 sheet metal, 0 concrete.") and a bar with the average health (each wall against its own max health).

Raising

Raising cost formula: planks = nails = ⌈(meters − 0.05) ÷ 4⌉ (a wall of up to 0.5 m costs nothing). Gain: 5 Carpentry XP per plank, plus 5. See Raising the walls.

Reinforcing

Base → BASE → Reinforce with sheet metal (200 health): ... or Reinforce with concrete (350 health): .... The button only appears if there's a wall to reinforce and only works inside the base.

  • The game automatically picks the standing wall with the weakest reinforcement (walls that are already concrete and fallen walls are ignored).
  • The material can come from the backpack and the chests.
  • When reinforced, the wall comes back with the full health of the new level.
  • Wood → sheet metal gives 20 Mechanics XP; sheet metal → concrete gives 20 Carpentry XP.
  • When there's nothing left to reinforce: "All standing walls are already concrete."

Repairing

Repair the weakest wall (1 plank + 1 nail), only inside the base:

  • picks the wall with the lowest health fraction;
  • restores 50 + 5 × Carpentry level health, without going over its reinforcement's maximum;
  • costs 1 plank + 1 nail and gives 10 Carpentry XP;
  • also repairs knocked-down walls (health 0), which hold again.

A concrete wall at 0 health needs 7 repairs with no Carpentry level (350 ÷ 50), or 4 with Carpentry 10 (100 per repair).

What knocks down walls

Walls only lose health in the night attack and when the weekly horde passes. Zombies on the action screen don't hit walls: with no path to you, they look for the nearest intact door or window (up to 40 m) and force their way through there. A knocked-down wall (health 0) disappears from the action screen and leaves the base open.

How to place

  1. At the base, open the action screen and tap the hammer (build mode).
  2. BUILD (door, window, furniture) → Door or Window.
  3. Get close to a wall and aim at it: the opening's ghost snaps to the wall closest to the front of the figure (up to 2 m).
  4. Tap the check mark to place it.

Rules for opening a door or window in a side:

  • The wall must be raised and standing ("Raise that wall first.", "That wall fell.").
  • It can't be a building wall ("That wall belongs to a building.").
  • The wall must be at least the width of the opening + 0.6 m, and the opening stays at least 0.3 m from each corner ("Too close to the corner.").
  • Two openings on the same side stay at least 0.3 m from each other ("Too close to another opening.").
The first doorThe first door you place in the character's life brings the crow: "A crow landed on your door. It stays."

Opening and closing the door

Get close to the door and tap OPEN DOOR / CLOSE DOOR. The main rule:

Open door = open baseAn open door lets everyone through, zombies too, and the base is no longer closed. In the night attack, each open door counts as a wall on the ground (and opens the way to chest losses). Broken doors and windows (health 0) become a hole in the wall and count the same.

A closed door holds like a wall and blocks vision. When open, the door leaf rests against the inside of the base.

Nailing planks over the window

Near an intact window without planks: NAIL PLANKS. It costs 2 planks + 2 nails, adds 60 max health (40 → 100) and leaves the window at full health right away. Only once per window ("Already has planks.").

Repairing a door or window

Near the damaged (or broken) opening: REPAIR (N%) or REPAIR. It costs 1 plank + 1 nail, comes back at full health and, if it's a door, it ends up closed. Gives 6 Carpentry XP. The label above shows the state ("Door · 72%", "Boarded window · 40%", "Broken door").

Removing a door or window

In build mode, near the opening: Remove. The wall closes again and the opening's cost goes back to the backpack (plus 2 planks if the window had planks nailed on).

Zombies against doors and windows

On the action screen, a zombie with no path to you walks to the nearest intact door or window (up to 40 m) and pounds on it:

  • each blow takes 5 to 9 health and makes noise (8 m), drawing anyone nearby;
  • door falling: "door fell!" and 18 m of noise; window: "glass broke!" and 25 m of noise;
  • at another player's base, the zombie pounds and makes noise but takes no health.

In the night attack, about 1 in 3 blows (35%) goes to a random closed door or window, and windows take 30% more damage. See Night attack.
