Base

How to mark out, raise, expand, defend and abandon your base, alone or in a group.

The base is a closed polygon that you mark out on the real map. Each side of the polygon is a wall: either a wall you raise with planks and nails (it has health and can fall in the night attack), or the wall of a real building (free and indestructible). Inside it go the furniture, the chests, the garden and the kitchen. You only have one base at a time.

The base menu is under the Base icon on the map, with the BASE tab (walls, outposts, rest, garden, abandon) and the CHESTS tab (all chests together).

Privacy notice

The first time you tap Mark out base, the game shows the notice "Your base is visible": in the online game, your base's area shows up for anyone who passes nearby (without your name). The game explicitly asks you not to build your base where you live.

To play from home, the game's own recommendation is to set up an outpost there: only you see outposts, and from them you go to the base through the action screen. The notice appears only once (buttons Cancel and Got it).

What others seeOther survivors within 300 m receive your base's layout from the server: the walls (health and whether they're raised), the doors and windows, and the placed furniture. Never your nickname, and never what's in the chests.

Marking out the base

  1. Start the session (you can't mark out without a session: the button shows as Mark out base (start the session)).
  2. Open Base and tap Mark out base. A 1 m grid appears on the map.
  3. Tap the grid to place the corners. Each corner must be within 60 m of you.
  4. Tap the 1st corner (less than 1.5 m from it) or the Mark out base button on the bar to close it. Use Undo point to remove the last corner and Cancel to give up.
  5. Then raise each wall with planks and nails (see below).

Marking out is free: the area becomes yours the moment it closes. The build bar shows how many corners you've placed, the current area against the limit, and how many planks and nails the walls will cost.

Grid rules

  • The grid has 1 m squares and is aligned with the nearest building (the building's corner within 40 m is a grid node). With no building nearby, it follows north.
  • Only right angles: each new corner is pulled onto the same row or column as the previous one.
  • A corner in the same direction as the previous side stretches the wall instead of creating a new corner. Going back over the same wall is refused ("Don't go back over the same wall.").
  • Walls can't cross each other, and you can't mark the same corner twice.
  • When closing, if the last corner isn't aligned with the first, the game adds the missing "elbow" on its own. Corners in the middle of a straight line are removed. If it can't be closed at a right angle, you get "Can't close at a right angle from here. Undo a corner."
  • At least 3 corners are needed.

Building walls

A whole side lying against a building's wall (every point of the side within 1.5 m of the building's wall) becomes a building wall: it costs no material, starts out "raised", has no health and never falls. The build bar reminds you: "Hug the buildings: building walls are free." A base with only building walls is already closed the moment it's marked out. You can't open a door or window in a building wall, nor push it to expand.

Formula: limit = 50 + 10 × kits + 15 × (group members − 1). A base larger than the limit is refused ("Too big: X m² (your limit is Y m²). Base expansion kits raise the limit.").

The Expansion kit is extremely rare: it isn't on any loot list; it drops with a 0.5% chance per place searched inside (rolled once per place, in the interior furniture), and whoever lights a Rescue flare (medium) has a 50% chance of finding one in the crate (the other half is an Outpost radio). Using the kit (Use in the backpack) adds 10 m² to your limit; in a group, the kit goes to the group's base.

Expanding the base (pushing a wall)

With area to spare, you enlarge the base by pushing a wall outward, 1 m at a time:

  1. At the base, open the action screen and tap the hammer (build mode).
  2. Get within 1.5 m of a wall (raised or only marked out).
  3. Tap EXPAND (push the wall 1 m).

The two neighboring walls get longer and you only pay for their extra wood (planks and nails in equal amounts, calculated by the 1-per-4-m rule). Furniture and doors stay where they are. You can't push when:

  • the wall or one of its neighbors is a building wall;
  • the new area goes over the limit ("Over the limit... An expansion kit gives +10 m² (and each group member, +15).");
  • the walls would cross, or a neighbor would be shorter than 1 m;
  • the new edge is less than 30 m from another base;
  • a door or window on the neighboring wall would end up less than 0.3 m from the corner;
  • a piece of furniture is in the way of the new wall ("... in the way of the wall: remove or move it first.");
  • you're standing less than 0.6 m from where the wall will go ("Step aside...").

Each expansion gives 3 Carpentry XP per plank spent. With an online account, the server checks the new area before accepting it.

Raising the walls

Once marked out, each real wall is still only "on the ground". While there's a wall that's only marked out, zombies get through there and the base isn't closed.

Wall lengthCost
Up to 0.5 mnothing
Every 4 m (or fraction)1 plank + 1 nail

Formula: planks = nails = ⌈(meters − 0.05) ÷ 4⌉. An 8 m wall costs 2 planks and 2 nails; an 8.1 m one, 3 of each.

  • On the action screen: get within 1.5 m of the marked-out wall and tap RAISE WALL (N plank + N nail).
  • In the panel: Base → BASE → Raise walls (...) raises all the ones your material covers (only inside the base).

Each raised wall starts as wood with 100 health and gives 5 Carpentry XP per plank + 5. When the last one goes up: "Last wall standing: the base is closed!". The material can come from the backpack and, inside the base, from the chests. Details on reinforcement, doors and windows in Walls, doors and windows.

Closed base

The base is closed when: all walls are raised, none has been knocked down (health 0), and no door is open or broken (a broken window also counts as a hole). Only a closed base truly protects you.

The yard

The yard is the strip up to 15 m beyond the edge. There you can place furniture (garden, barrel, campfire and anything else) and use it through the action screen. The yard isn't protected by the walls.

Sleeping at the base

Ending the session inside the base (by GPS) is sleeping: the menu shows Sleep (end here). With the app closed, tiredness drops to zero in about 8 h and health comes back at 5 per hour (with no bleeding or infection). With a bed placed, rest is 50% more effective and health comes back at 7.5 per hour. Ending at an outpost or in the tent also counts as sleeping, but without the bed bonus.

Group base

In a group there's only one base, shared:

  • Creating a group with a base: your base becomes the group's and the chests are uploaded to the server. Your expansion kits start counting for it.
  • Group without a base: whoever marks one out first makes the group's base.
  • The area limit gains +15 m² per member beyond the first; kits used by any member go to the group's base.
  • Walls, furniture and chests are synced with the server every 15 s and right after any change. Everyone uses the same chests.
  • The night attack and the horde passing happen once for everyone: the server picks one phone to apply it and the result reaches the others.
  • The group base can't be abandoned while anyone else is in the group ("The base belongs to the group: you can only take it down when you are the only one left in the group.").
  • Leaving the group: the base and the chests stay with the group; you take nothing from them (the radio depot is still yours). If you were the last one, the group ends and the base stays with you.

Joining a group while having a base

When you join a group, your base is taken down. The game asks for confirmation ("Join the group?") and then:

  • everything that was in the chests goes to the radio depot, even if it goes over 300 kg (above the limit you can only take things out until you sort it);
  • the material from all raised walls, doors, windows (with the nailed planks: 2 planks + 2 nails) and furniture comes back, also to the depot;
  • the planted garden is lost and the base disappears from the server.

The final notice says how much the depot weighs and suggests going to an outpost to sort it.

Abandoning the base

Base → BASE → Abandon base (only inside the base). Confirmation: "Abandon the base?". When you abandon it:

  • what's in the chests goes entirely to the backpack (it can get heavy);
  • all the material comes back to the backpack: planks and nails from each raised wall, the cost of each door and window (plus 2 planks + 2 nails from each boarded window) and the cost of each piece of furniture;
  • the garden is lost and the night attack count starts over.

Sheet metal and concrete reinforcements are not returned (only the wall's wood). After that, just mark out again.
