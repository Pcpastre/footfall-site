Zombie: Brute

The boss: a huge zombie, with six times the health of a common zombie, a knockdown blow and a straight-line charge.

The boss: a huge zombie, with six times the health of a common zombie, a knockdown blow and a straight-line charge. It is not found on the street.

Behavior

On the action screen the Brute behaves like a large zombie, with these differences:

  • Blow: instead of the scratch, a blow dealing 8 to 14 damage (4 to 7 × 2) that throws you 1.1 m back and knocks you down for 1.2 s, plus 0.8 s to get up ("Brute's blow").
  • Bite: 12 to 20 damage (double).
  • Never grabs.
  • Charge: every so often, if you are more than 4 m and less than 14 m away with a clear line, it stops, growls and aims for 0.9 s ("IT ROARED! GET OUT OF THE WAY"), then runs in a straight line for up to 1.8 s at 2.8× its speed. The first charge can come after 6 s of fighting.
  • If the charge hits you: 8 to 14 damage, you are thrown 1.4 m and knocked down for 1.6 s, plus 0.8 s to get up ("RUN OVER!"). It waits 9 to 12 s to charge again.
  • If it hits a wall or furniture: it staggers for 1.3 s ("slammed into it") and waits 9 to 12 s. If it just misses, it waits 8 to 12 s.
  • Rage: below 30% health it becomes 20% faster.
  • Its legs hold up more: it takes 60% of max health in leg damage for it to fall and crawl. While crawling it doesn't charge.
  • Being knocked down still has a 5% chance of fracture and 35% of bruise, like any fall.

Where it appears

  • At the head of the last wave of the hard flare: the first zombie of the 5th wave.
  • In the antechamber of the last floor (Vault) of every bunker, guarding the vault door.
  • Never on the street, in the horde or inside ordinary places.

Loot

Every body has the common pocket: 45% chance of cloth rags, 0 to 2 things from the pocket list and a 6% chance of a stamp (see Bodies).

On top of that, the job-specific loot. Each row is rolled separately; the quantity, when there is one, goes from 1 to the maximum, with the same chance for each number.

Tips

TipWhen it stops and growls ("IT ROARED! GET OUT OF THE WAY"), step to the side: the charge goes straight and stops at the first wall.
TipLure the charge into a wall or furniture: it slams into it and staggers for 1.3 s, your moment to strike.
TipAiming for the legs takes 60% of max health in leg damage, but a crawling Brute doesn't charge and moves at 0.35 m/s.
TipBelow 30% health it becomes 20% faster.
TipIn the bunker antechamber, the floor's safe room is close by: fight with the armored door at your back.
