Garden

Garden beds, seeds, watering, growth time, the effect of Farming, and harvesting.

The garden is made of garden beds placed in the base or in the yard. Each bed takes a packet of seeds, grows in real time as long as it's watered, and gives garden vegetables at harvest. The panel is under Base → BASE → Garden (N/4 garden beds · Farming N).

Garden beds

RecipeCostWorkbenchSkillXPLimit
H01 · Garden bed2 planks + 2 nailsnoFarming154 beds per base

Make it under Backpack → CRAFT (inside the base) and place it with the hammer on the action screen (see Base furniture). The bed measures 1.2 × 1.2 m and can go in the yard (up to 15 m from the edge). To take it apart, it has to be empty.

Step by step

  1. Plant: Plant uses 1 seed packet (from the backpack or the chests). +8 Farming XP. Notice: "Planted. Water it every day so it grows."
  2. Water: Water uses 1 clean water bottle. Each watering lasts 24 hours. +3 Farming XP.
  3. Wait: the bed only grows during the hours covered by a watering. When dry, it stops growing (without losing what it has already grown).
  4. Harvest: when it's ready, the button becomes Harvest. The vegetables go into the backpack. +25 Farming XP.

All of this only works with you at the base or in the yard ("Only at the base or in the yard."), from the panel or with USE GARDEN BED on the action screen.

Real timeThe garden grows with the app closed too, as long as the last watering is still in effect. Watering right before sleeping guarantees 24 h of growth.

Growth time

Base: 72 hours (3 days) of watered growth. Farming speeds it up 5% per level:

hours = 72 × (1 − 0.05 × level), with the factor never below 0.4.

Harvest

There's only one crop in the game: the seed always becomes Garden vegetables.

vegetables = 3 + ⌊Farming level ÷ 2⌋

ItemWeightCaloriesWaterShelf life
Garden vegetables0.4 kg250 kcal100 ml4 days

The vegetables can be eaten raw or used in the kitchen: Cook vegetables (C03), Soup (C08) and Canned vegetables (C07, which last 3 months). They also feed the crow (6 bond).

On the action screen the bed appears empty, with sprouts, or ripe.

Where to get seeds and water

  • Seed packet: markets, construction sites, shops, houses, and the general store (5 per day).
  • Water bottle: looting, the general store, or boiling rainwater from the barrel (see Kitchen). Unboiled rainwater can't be used for watering.
GroupJoining a group takes down your base: the planted garden is lost. Taking apart a garden bed requires harvesting first.
