Kitchen

Campfire and wood stove, how to light them, every cooking recipe, shelf life, rainwater, boiling and filtering.

Cooking turns raw or meager food into food that goes further, and turns rainwater into clean water. Every cooking recipe requires a lit fire at the base: a campfire or a wood stove placed there.

The two fires

FireRecipeCostWorkbenchAt night
CampfireM053 rocks + 2 planksnoThe light draws zombies within 100 m and adds 3 attackers to the night attack
Wood stoveM061 sheet metal + 1 iron pipe + 2 nailsyes (Mechanics, 30 XP)Enclosed: almost no light, draws no one

Lighting

  1. On the action screen, get close to the placed fire and tap LIGHT CAMPFIRE or LIGHT WOOD STOVE.
  2. It needs a lighter (not used up) and uses 1 plank of firewood.
  3. The fire stays lit for 30 minutes (real time). While lit, it glows on the action screen and the button becomes COOK.
  4. COOK opens the CRAFT tab with the fire recipes at the top and the notice "On the fire: cooking goes further and the food lasts again."

Without a lighter: "Needs a lighter.". Without a plank: "Needs 1 firewood plank.". Trying to cook while not at the fire: "Only at the base's lit fire (campfire or stove): walk up to it and tap USE."

Campfire at nightWhen you light the campfire, the game warns: "Campfire lit (30 min). At night the light draws zombies from far away." Zombies within 100 m of the base that aren't chasing you go to it to see the light. To cook at night without drawing anyone, use the wood stove.

Shelf life

Perishable food spoils after its shelf life, in real time. Eating spoiled food gives only half the calories, removes 8 health and leaves you nauseous for 2 hours.

Rainwater

The rain barrel collects 1 bottle every 10 min of rain (up to 20). When you use the barrel, each empty bottle in your backpack becomes a Rainwater (not boiled): "Filled N bottle(s) with rainwater. Boil it on the fire before drinking."

ItemWaterRisk
Rainwater (not boiled)500 ml1 in 3 (33%) causes nausea: −4 health and 2 h nauseous
Water bottle500 mlnone

When nauseous, you get the "Nauseous" status and max stamina drops 15%. Drinking any bottle gives you back the empty bottle.

Other places to cook

Only your base's fires can cook. Eating at the base table gives 10% more calories.
