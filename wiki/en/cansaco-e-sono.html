Fatigue and sleep

How fatigue rises and falls, its levels and effects, and how much you rest and heal sleeping at the base, in a bed, in a sleeping bag or out on the street.

Fatigue goes from 0 to 100% and shows up in the Fatigue bar on the Health tab. It rises as you walk and run and only really drops when you stop or end the session. In Footfall, sleeping is ending the session: the time with the app closed is your sleep time, and where you ended determines how restful it is.

How fatigue rises

ActivityFatigue
Walking (up to 3 m/s)+1.5 per km
Running (above 3 m/s)+9 per km (6 per km, and each meter running counts as 1.5)
Anti-anxiety pills+10 (+25 if already under the effect of another)
Morphine+15

In a vehicle (above about 25 km/h for more than 3 s) the distance doesn't count, so it doesn't tire you either.

How fatigue drops

SituationRecovery
Standing still (below 0.4 m/s) with the session active−0.5 per minute (−30 per hour)
Ground coffee−10 instantly
Drip coffee−25 instantly

Max stamina starts at 100 and loses 0.4 per point of fatigue before trait multipliers. The result never goes below 30.

Sleeping

To sleep, open the menu and tap Sleep (end here) when you're in a safe place, or End session outside one. The game confirms: Sleep at the base? / Sleep at the outpost? with the Sleep button, or End here? with the End button. Ending anywhere keeps everything you're carrying.

Safe places to sleep are: inside your base, at one of your outposts or in your pitched tent.

  • Bed: it must be a piece of furniture Bed placed in your base, and you must have ended inside the base (not at an outpost). It's 50% more restful than the base without a bed.
  • Sleeping bag: it just needs to be in your backpack when you end outside the base, outpost and tent. "You slept in the sleeping bag: better rest than the ground, but not like a bed."
  • Health: only comes back while sleeping if you're not bleeding or carrying a zombie infection.
  • Mind: sleeping with base rest, stress drops 30 and sadness 5 per hour; with normal rest, 10 and 1 per hour. The column shows the hour multiplier: in a bed each hour counts as 1.5. See Mind.

The messages when you end say where you slept: "You slept at the base. Full rest while the app is closed.", "You slept in the tent. ...", "You slept at the outpost. ...".

What happens while you sleep

  • Hunger and thirst keep dropping, but stop at 11% (see Needs).
  • Treated wounds keep healing in real time (without the stretcher bonus).
  • Bleeding doesn't drain health with the app closed, and wounds don't catch common infections.
  • The zombie infection keeps running and can kill you before you come back.
  • Your base can suffer night attacks.
How much to sleepAt the base without a bed, 8 h closed clears fatigue and restores 40 health. With a bed, just over 5 h already clears fatigue, and 8 h restores 60 health. The bed ("Bed" recipe, 3 planks, 2 nails and 4 rags, at the workbench) is one of the first pieces of furniture worth building.
