Zombie: Doctor

A doctor or nurse in a white coat.

A doctor or nurse in a white coat. No protection, but 12% faster than a common zombie. Their pockets are full of dressings and medicine.

Shotgun blasts ignore the uniform's protection. The full multipliers are in Melee combat and Firearms.

Behavior

On the map it's like any zombie: it can be asleep, wandering or downed, sees in a 100° cone and hears your noise (see Zombies). The uniform only changes the fight and the loot.

On the action screen it's 12% faster: it reaches you before the others in the group.

Where it appears

  • Near and inside health places (pharmacies, hospitals, clinics): 25% of zombies within 50 m.
  • On the street: 12% of street specials.

Loot

Every body has the common pocket: a 45% chance of cloth rags, 0 to 2 things from the pocket list and a 6% chance of a stamp (see Bodies).

On top of that, the trade loot. Each row is rolled separately; the quantity, when there is one, goes from 1 to the maximum, with the same chance for each number.
