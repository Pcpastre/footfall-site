Old Jorge

The doorman's memory: boarding up windows and doors, storing things in the chest and making repairs.

Old Jorge is the memory of a building's doorman on the first day. It teaches the basics of defending a place: planks and nails, barricades, the chest and repairs.

Profile
ProtagonistThe building's doorman · day 0, morning
Where to find itThe book Memory: Mr. Jorge (a brown-covered notebook in a doorman's handwriting), inside houses, with a 0.6% chance per place
Challenge typeBoard up a window and door, store things in the chest, repair the gate; defense
WeatherDaytime, no rain
ProfessionUnemployed, no traits
Starting kit2 Water bottle, 2 Can of beans, 1 Bread; wearing the Daypack
RewardDoorman outfit (dress shirt, dark trousers, the building's cap and a bunch of keys on the belt)
ScoreboardKills

Gameplay tips

  • The memory's actions appear as buttons when you get close: nail planks, close the door, store in the chest, repair. Each nailing shows its cost (1 plank · 1 nail).
  • Planks and nails are limited: search the furniture before you start nailing.
  • Boarding up works the same as at your real base: a window with planks holds out much longer. See Walls, doors and windows.
  • A zombie with no path to you forces the nearest door or window: keep an eye on the ones losing health.
  • The kit's food and water are in the backpack: in a fight, taking something out of the backpack takes time and makes noise.
  • If you go down, FROM HERE restarts from the last checkpoint.
