Skills

The 10 skills, how each one gains experience, the XP formula and what each level changes.

Skills go up with use, from level 0 to 10, and are all lost when the character dies ("They go up with use. If you die, you lose it all."). You start with the levels given by your profession and by some traits. Progress appears on the character panel, Skills tab: ten little level squares and a thin bar with the XP to the next one.

Experience formula

Going from level N to N+1 takes 60 × (N + 1) XP points. Leftover XP carries over to the next level, and a big gain can raise several levels at once. At level 10 the skill stops gaining XP. When you level up, the notice "Level up: <skill> <level>" appears.

Each skill in detail

Melee weapons

XP: 4 per hit landed with a melee weapon, counted at the end of each fight; 10 per guard when you win the takeover of a point.

Effects per level: melee damage starts at 75% and adds 5 points per level (level 5 = 100% without traits). The critical window grows 2% per level. Each hit has a 3% chance per level of not using the weapon's durability. The chance that a head strike hits the shoulder drops 3 points per level (base 40%, minimum 5%).

Strength

XP: 6 per zombie killed and 2 per shove, counted at the end of the fight; fighting with fists, hits (4 each) also go to Strength instead of Melee weapons.

Effects per level: +3% melee damage; +0.5 kg of pocket capacity; throwing on the map reaches 18 m + 1.5 m per level; the shove costs 20 stamina minus 1 per Strength level (minimum 12), pushes harder and knocks down more often (45% + 4% per level, up to 80%); to break free of a grab, each level helps 8% more.

Stamina

XP: walking, 1 every 150 m; running (above 3 m/s), 1 every 40 m. Vehicles don't count. In a fight, +1 per hit landed.

Effects per level: +3% max stamina (added to the stamina trait multiplier), +5% to how fast it comes back, −4% stamina cost when running on the action screen.

Stealth

XP: 1 every 15 m walked below 1.8 m/s with an awake zombie that isn't chasing you within 40 m; 3 per object thrown on the map (bottle, firework, alarm clock...).

Effects per level: −4% to your footstep noise (total noise never goes below 30% of normal) and −4% to the distance at which zombies spot you on the map.

Effects per level: each wall repair restores 50 + 5 per level of health; recipes require a minimum level; in every carpentry or mechanics recipe there's a 3% chance the tool slips and gives you a deep cut on the hand, minus 0.5 points per level of the recipe's skill (zero from level 6 on).

Mechanics

XP: 20 for reinforcing a wooden wall to sheet metal. Recipes: Makeshift spear 30 (level 1), Repair weapon in hand 20, Repair weapon at the workbench 30 (level 1), Wood stove 30, Floodlight 30 (level 1), Improvised silencer 15, Reload shells 20 (level 1), Blade on the tip of the long weapon 15, Flashlight mounted on the weapon 15.

Effects per level: each weapon repair restores 5% more; unlocks level 1 recipes; reduces the risk of cutting your hand (like Carpentry).

Farming

XP: planting 8, watering 3, harvesting 25; recipes Garden bed 15 and Canned vegetables 12.

Effects per level: (see Garden) the garden bed needs 72 watered hours to be ready, minus 5% per level (level 10 = 36 h); each harvest gives 3 vegetables + 1 every 2 levels (level 10 = 8).

Firearms

XP: 6 per shot that hits, counted at the end of the fight.

Effects per level: single-shot spread drops 0.5° per level (never below 1°) and the shotgun's 0.4°; the chance of hitting the head rises 4 points per level when aiming for the head (3 without aiming); reloading gets 5% faster. Below level 6, Strong panic removes 15% and Extreme panic 25% of shot damage; from level 6 on, it no longer does.

Tailoring

XP: sewing a piece of clothing 5; pitching the tent 6; recipes Sleeping bag 35 (level 1), Tent 80 (level 2) and Patch the tarp 25.

Effects per level: clothes wear 5% less with each hit or bite they block (level 10 = half the wear).

Recipes that give no XP

The full list of recipes, with ingredients, is in Recipes.

Recipes without a skill give no experience at all: Campfire, Molotov cocktail, Tin can wire, Covered pit, Extract the gunpowder from a firework, Biodiesel, Wrap the weapon's handle, all food cooked on the fire (except Canned vegetables), Boil water and Filter water through cloth. Rebuilding a piece of furniture you dismantled also gives no XP.

Lost on deathAll XP and all levels are gone when the character dies. The new character starts again from the profession and trait levels. See Death.
