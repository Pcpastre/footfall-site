Mind

Panic, stress, sadness, boredom, cigarette addiction, phobias and hemophobia: what makes them rise, the levels, the effects and how to bring them down.

The survivor's mind, in the style of Project Zomboid, has four meters: panic (the fright of the moment), stress, sadness and boredom. The last three appear as bars in the Head section of the Health tab; panic appears as a moodle. They all go from 0 to 100.

Panic

Levels

Panic from the map carries over to the action screen and comes back to the map at the end of the fight. The levels are the same in both places.

What makes it rise

On the map: each zombie within 40 m that you can see or that is chasing you adds, per second, 1.5 × (1 − d/40)² (3 × if it's chasing you), where d is the distance. The closer they are and the more zombies there are, the faster it rises. Inside a closed base (walls standing and doors shut), zombies don't cause panic.

On the action screen:

CausePanic
A zombie that noticed you within 10 m, seen or sensed10 × (1 − d/10)² per second, added per zombie
A zombie that suddenly appears less than 6 m awayup to +8 from the scare
Taking damage+6
Being grabbed+10
Being knocked down+15
Getting wounded, with Hemophobic+12
Cigarette withdrawal+10 at the start of the fight

FactorMultiplier
Brave / Cowardly / Desensitized×0.3 / ×2 / ×0
Stress×(1 + stress/133): 75 stress = +56%
Anti-anxiety pills×0.4
Beta blocker×0.5

How to bring it down

With no zombie nearby, panic drops on its own: 1.5 per second on the map and 2.5 per second on the action screen, multiplied by how used to it you are (from ×1 on the first day up to ×3 at 60 days alive). On the action screen, each shove takes off 10. Items:

ItemPanic
Bottle of cachaçaClears it
Anti-anxiety pills−40
Pack of cigarettes (Smoker only)−20
Beta blocker−15

Effects

  • Map: the vision cone narrows 0.25° per point of panic (down to −25°).
  • Action screen, vision: the cone narrows 0.5° per point (never below 110°).
  • Melee attack (see Melee combat): −10% damage at Panicked, −20% at Strong panic, −30% at Extreme panic; the critical window shrinks and the critical chance drops; the chance of missing the head goes up.
  • Shove: pushes and knocks down less (−8% force and −10% knockdown chance per level).
  • Shooting: spread grows with panic; below Firearms 6, Strong panic takes off 15% and Extreme panic 25% of the damage.
  • Treatment: sutures and shard removal fail 8% more at Panicked, 16% at Strong panic, 24% at Extreme panic (see Treatment).
  • Adrenaline junkie: the trait turns high panic into an advantage: +10% speed at Strong panic and +25% at Extreme panic.

Phobias

The Claustrophobic and Agoraphobic traits create a panic floor of 35 in certain places:

PhobiaWhere
ClaustrophobicWith 4 or more buildings within 15 m of you (alleys, closed blocks)
AgoraphobicWhen the nearest street (within 15 m) is wide, 9 m or more (avenues and main streets)

On the map, in these places panic doesn't drop below half the floor (17.5, Mild panic). On an action screen opened there, panic rises on its own up to 35 (Panicked) even with no zombie. Desensitized has no phobias.

Hemophobia

With the Hemophobic trait:

  • each treatment step done in the Health tab adds +10 panic;
  • the quick dressing adds +35;
  • each wound taken on the action screen adds +12 × your panic multiplier (at least +6).

Since panic makes sutures worse, first treat the wounds that can't fail (bandaging, cleaning) and leave the suture for when the panic goes down.

Desensitized gains no stress or sadness from fights and wounds (the bite still counts).

How to bring it down

  • With no active cause, stress drops on its own: −0.1 per second inside the base (6 per minute), −0.02 outside (1.2 per minute).
  • Sleeping: −30 per hour with base rest (base, outpost, tent), −10 per hour elsewhere; in a bed each hour counts as 1.5, in a sleeping bag 1.2.
  • Items: Anti-anxiety pills −35, cigarette −25 (Smoker), Cachaça −15, Chocolate −5.

What makes it rise

  • Very stressed: +0.01 per second.
  • Very bored or worse: +0.02 per second.
  • Bites, wounds, kills and spoiled food (events table above).

How to bring it down

  • Inside the base, with nothing making it rise: −0.01 per second (0.6 per minute).
  • Sleeping: −5 per hour with base rest, −1 per hour elsewhere.
  • Antidepressant: starts working 1 h after taking it and takes off about 40 per hour until 24 h after the dose, also with the app closed.
  • Items: Chocolate −15, Comic book −10, Drip coffee −8, Hot soup −8, Ground coffee −5, Cachaça −5.

Smoking

Only for those with the Smoker trait. After 4 real hours without smoking (counting from character creation, the first time), withdrawal sets in, with the Craving a smoke moodle:

  • max stamina ×0.9;
  • +0.03 stress per second;
  • +10 panic when entering the action screen ("Craving a smoke: more nervous").

The Smoke button on the Pack of cigarettes uses up 1 pack, resets the clock, and takes off 20 panic and 25 stress.

Getting used to it

The more days the character has been alive, the less panic frightens them: panic gain drops 1% per day (down to −60% at 60 days) and the speed at which it passes increases up to 3 times. This adjustment is lost with death, along with the rest of the character.
