A group brings together up to 10 survivors. Members see on the panel where each one is and how far away, send quick alerts with their location, chat, trade items side by side and share a base with shared chests. The panel is under the Group icon on the map. Everything in a group requires an online account: with no connection, the panel shows "No connection to the server. Sign in to use the group." and the Log in to account button.

Create or join

ActionHowWhat happens to your base
CreateType the New group name (up to 24 letters; empty becomes "Group") and tap Create groupIf you have a base, it becomes the group's base and the chests are uploaded to the server ("Group created. Code: ... Your base now belongs to the group.")
JoinType the Group code (6 characters) and tap Join groupIf you have a base, it is dismantled: chests and materials go to the radio depot and the garden is lost (the game asks for confirmation)

You can only be in one group at a time ("You're already in a group."). Inside the group, the panel shows the code with a copy button and how many members there are (N/10).

NicknameWithout a nickname, the panel suggests: "Tip: set a nickname in Settings → Appearance so the group knows who you are." The nickname is 3 to 16 characters long (letters, numbers and _, no spaces).

While you're in a session, each member also shows their distance from you.

Privacy

  • Your nickname is private: no one sees it on the map or above your character; only your group. Outside the group, others only recognize you by your clothes and colors.
  • The group sees where you are (panel and alerts). Other survivors never see you on the map: they only appear to you, and you to them, on the action screen, when they're close, with no name.
  • No one outside sees names ("No one outside sees names.").

Quick alerts (pings)

Three buttons send an alert with your location to the whole group (you need to be in a session: "Alerts use your location: start the session."):

ButtonText
hordeHORDE HERE
lootLOOT HERE
helpHELP!

Whoever receives it sees an alert with the nickname and distance ("Ana: HELP! 340 m away"), the phone vibrates and, if they have a crow, it comes flying in from the teammate's direction. The alert stays marked on the map for 10 minutes.

Chat

The Message to the group field accepts up to 200 characters. The panel shows the last 30 messages (the game keeps up to 100), with who sent them and how long ago. Quick alerts appear in the same list, highlighted.

Group roles

Each member can choose a role (it only applies while in a group). The role gives +25% experience in its skills (+50% with the Leader trait) and an extra effect:

Trades

Side by side, with the group

A member in a session within 40 m of you gets the Swap button on the panel.

With anyone nearby

Menu (...) → Trade with someone here: "Both in a session, within 40 m. One creates the code, the other types it." One taps Create code and shows the 6-digit code (valid for 10 min); the other types it into Other player's code and taps Log in.

How trading works

  1. Each one chooses what to offer and taps Offer. Changing the offer removes both players' "ok".
  2. Both tap Accept.
  3. On the final screen ("Check carefully: this is the final screen. Once both confirm, the trade happens."), each one confirms with Accept trade or backs out with Decline.
  • At the final confirmation, your items leave your inventory until the trade closes: you can't use, store or sell them while the other person decides. If the trade is canceled or changes, they come back.
  • Each side shows the value by the price guide (~N stamp(s)). If you're giving much more than you get, you see "Careful: you're giving much more than you get (by guide price)."
  • Stamps can't be traded ("Stamps stay in your wallet: to sell for stamps, use the market.").
  • What counts is what's loose in your backpack and, at the base, in the chests.

Group base and chests

  • The group has one base. If no one has one, whoever marks out first makes the group's base ("The group doesn't have a base yet: whoever marks out first makes the group's base (up to N m²).").
  • Area limit: 50 m² + 10 per expansion kit used in the group + 15 per member beyond the first.
  • Everyone works on the same walls, furniture and chests; changes are synced with the server every 15 s and right after you make them.
  • The night attack and the horde count once for everyone (see Night attack).
  • Strongholds taken by a member belong to the group (see Strongholds).
  • The weekly radio mission is the same for the whole group.
  • The group base can only be dismantled when only you are left.

Leaving the group

Leave group asks for confirmation:

  • with more people: "The base and chests belong to the group: you take nothing from them. The radio vault is still yours."
  • if you're the last one: "You're the last one: the group ends and the base stays with you."

Memorial

The Memorial section (Who in the group is down) lists the members who died, with days alive, kills, kilometers and cause. When empty: "No one yet. Let's keep it that way."
