The most common zombie in the city: people who turned without any uniform. No protection, no job-specific loot, just whatever was left in their pockets.

Shotgun blasts ignore the uniform's protection. The full multipliers are in Melee combat and Firearms.

Behavior

On the map it is like any zombie: it can be dormant, wandering or downed, sees in a 100° cone and hears your noise (see Zombies). The uniform only changes the fight and the loot.

Where it appears

  • On any street and inside any place: it is what's left after the rolls for the special ones (see Zombies).
  • In the weekly horde, in the flare waves and in bunkers, alongside the special ones.
  • The zombie with the face of a dead survivor, standing on top of the pile of their belongings, is always a common zombie.

Loot

Every body has the common pocket: 45% chance of cloth rags, 0 to 2 things from the pocket list and a 6% chance of a stamp (see Bodies).

It has no job-specific loot and no outfit.
