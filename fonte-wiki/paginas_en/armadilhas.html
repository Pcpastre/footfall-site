Traps are base furniture that you place on the ground, inside the walls or in the yard (up to 15 m from the edge). They work in two ways: on the action screen, when a zombie steps on them, and in the night attack, catching some of the attackers before they reach the walls.

Stakes

A row of stakes measuring 1.2 × 0.6 m. When a zombie walks over it:

  • it takes 12 damage ("stakes!" appears) and adds 12 to its accumulated leg damage;
  • if the leg damage goes over 60% of its max health, it becomes a crawler;
  • if its health reaches 0, it dies right there;
  • it spends 1 of the 6 uses.

Rearm: walk up to it and tap USE STAKES. It costs 1 plank + 2 nails (from the backpack or the chests), goes back to 6 uses and gives 6 Carpentry XP. Already full: "Already set (6 times)."

Covered pit

A 1 × 1 m pit disguised with planks and cloth. A zombie that steps on it falls ("fell into the pit") and stays down for triple the normal fall time plus the time to get up: 5 × 3 + 1.2 = 16.2 seconds without attacking. It spends 1 of the 3 uses.

Rearm: USE COVERED PIT. It costs 1 plank + 2 cloth rags, goes back to 3 uses and gives 6 Carpentry XP.

Tin can wire

A low 1.6 m wire with cans hanging from it. When a zombie passes, the cans rattle and "cans!" appears above it: you hear that someone has arrived. It never runs out and doesn't need rearming ("The can alarm doesn't need anything."). USE TIN CAN WIRE only explains: "Tin can wire: when a zombie passes, the cans rattle and you hear it."

Rules on the action screen

  • A zombie triggers the trap when it enters the trap's area.
  • After triggering a trap, the same zombie can't trigger another for 3 seconds.
  • Uses spent on the action screen stay spent at the base (even when the scene restarts while roaming freely).

Rules in the night attack

In the night attack and when the horde passes, traps act before the walls:

  1. The game goes through each placed stake and pit that still has uses.
  2. Each one catches up to one third of the attackers not yet caught (integer division), limited to the uses it has.
  3. The uses for those caught are spent.
  4. Only the ones left over hit the walls, doors and windows.

Example: 9 attackers, a full pit (3 uses) and full stakes (6 uses). The pit catches ⌊9 ÷ 3⌋ = 3 (left with 0 uses); the stakes catch ⌊6 ÷ 3⌋ = 2 (left with 4). That leaves 4 zombies hitting.

The tin can wire doesn't catch anyone, but if at least one is placed, each hit of the attack is multiplied by 0.85 (you were warned). The same goes for the watchtower (×0.85) and for a lit floodlight (×0.8); the three multiply together.

The attack notice shows how many fell into the traps ("..., 5 in the traps."). The base panel reminds you: "Traps catch some zombies in the attack."

TipPut stakes and pits where the zombies will pass: in front of doors and windows and along the yard paths. Rearm after every night: a trap with no uses left catches no one in the next attack.
