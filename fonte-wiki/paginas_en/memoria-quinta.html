Thursday duty is the memory of a military police officer at the neighborhood police station, on the first day. It teaches firearms: ammo in your pockets versus in your backpack, reloading, the shotgun, aiming for the head and the noise of gunfire.

Gameplay tips

  • Ammo in your pockets reloads instantly; in the backpack, it takes time to find and makes noise. Keep your ammo in your pockets.
  • Every shot draws zombies from a wide radius: shoot when it's worth it and use the baton on stragglers.
  • Aiming for the head deals high damage but misses more; aiming for the legs knocks them down and they crawl.
  • Ammo is limited: search the station. Locked doors need a key or force.
  • The shotgun spreads buckshot: good against several up close, bad at range.
  • If you fall, FROM HERE restarts from the last checkpoint.
