The action screen has three control modes. Choose one in Settings → CONTROLS. The mode also applies in memories.

The screen halves

There is no attack button: the whole screen is the button. A quick tap:

  • on the left half: shove;
  • on the right half: strike (with a firearm, shoot).

Holding and dragging the screen moves the camera to see more of one side; Center (at the top) brings it back. Taps don't attack while a panel is open or in the base's build mode.

Pressing strike before the weapon is ready delays the next strike by 0.15 s ("too soon!"). Standing still and not aiming, the strike turns by itself toward the nearest zombie within 1.6× the weapon's reach.

Simple mode and the FIGHT button

The FIGHT button turns on the fighting stance and then shows FIGHTING, in amber.

  • In the stance you automatically face the nearest zombie (with a firearm, the nearest one you can see within 14 m; with a melee weapon, within 2.2× the weapon's reach) and move sideways, at 60% speed.
  • The stance counts as aiming all the time: more accurate strikes with a chance of critical (see Melee combat).
  • Tapping the screen outside the stance turns the stance on and strikes immediately.
  • Running turns the stance off ("who runs doesn't fight").
  • It also turns off by itself after 6 s with no zombie within 12 m ("relaxed").
  • When off, the button pulses red when an alert zombie is within 8 m.

Two sticks mode

  • The right stick (red) aims: wherever it points, your character looks and strikes.
  • Holding the right stick turns on the fighting stance while you hold it and for 0.4 s after you let go.
  • With a firearm, the aim you set stays in effect for 1.5 s even while walking (you can strafe while shooting).
  • Striking without aiming (right stick released) is worse: damage ×0.85, a 25% chance of a glancing blow (half damage) and a higher chance of missing the head.

Automatic mode

You only move. Your character faces the nearest zombie and:

  • strikes by itself when a zombie is within the weapon's reach;
  • shoots by itself when it sees a zombie within 14 m (even with longer-range weapons);
  • shoves by itself when on the ground or grabbed, and when there are 3 or more zombies within 1.1 m (if it has stamina for the shove + 5);
  • the shove hits all around (360°), not just in front.

The price: the interval between automatic strikes is 1.35× longer, further multiplied by 1 + panic ÷ 200 (with panic at 100, about 2× normal) and by the reflex rate (Fast reflexes ×0.9, Slow reflexes ×1.1), it hits 15% weaker and has only a 4% base critical chance. Automatic bursts come out shot by shot, at the weapon's rate of fire.

Taps on the screen halves also work in this mode.

Walking and running

StickSpeed
Up to 72% of its travelwalking, proportional, up to 2.0 m/s
Beyond 72% (the edge)running, 4.2 m/s

Running needs more than 5 stamina and uses 10 per second (4% less per Stamina level). You can't run while crouched, on the ground or grabbed. Walking uses 2 stamina per second (proportional to the stick). With less than 10 stamina you walk at 70%.

While running, any unaware zombie within 5 m (times the footstep multiplier) notices you instantly. Other speed multipliers: load (see Backpack and weight), pain, fracture, cluttered floor and the Adrenaline junkie trait (+10% with strong panic, +25% at extreme).

On the ground, moving the stick makes you get up faster.

Crouching

The CROUCH button (it becomes STANDING) is on the left, above the stick.

  • You move at 45% speed and can't run.
  • Unaware zombies only see you within 4.5 m (standing, 7 m) and take twice as long to notice.
  • Shots spread 1 degree less.
  • Armed guards hit less (−10 percentage points).
  • Being knocked down puts you back on your feet.

Target selector

In the top right corner, below the weapon, the target icon changes where you aim with each tap, in this order: BODY → LEGS → HEAD → BODY.

TargetEffect
HEADhigh damage, but can miss and hit the shoulder; a shot aimed at the head spreads 2 degrees more
BODYnormal damage, no risk (the zombie's vest absorbs part of it)
LEGSless damage, but builds up leg damage until it falls and crawls

A downed or crawling zombie is always hit in the head. The numbers are in Melee combat and Firearms.

Other on-screen buttons

  • Weapon (top right corner): tap to open the list of weapons you can grab right now (back and belt) and switch. It shows durability (melee weapon) or rounds (firearm). Switching takes 0.7 s (0.3 s for fists), without attacking.
  • Build (hammer, base only): turns on build mode; in it, taps don't attack.
  • Exit: exit icon; see Action screen.
  • Throw: next to crouch, with the name and how many are left; holding it switches the item. See Throwing.
  • Heart (top left corner): tap to see health, stamina, panic and the mode.
  • Context buttons (they appear near things): RUMMAGE, SEARCH BODY, FORCE DOOR, DISMANTLE, GO UP/GO DOWN, ENTER: name, USE.
