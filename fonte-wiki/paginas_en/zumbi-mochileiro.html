Behavior

On the map he's like any zombie: he can be dormant, wandering or fallen, sees in a 100° cone and hears your noise (see Zombies). The uniform only changes combat and loot.

He only appears through the street specials roll (which also applies near and inside places when the trade roll doesn't hit).

Where he appears

  • Through the street roll: he's the most common street special (20% of specials, which are 7% of zombies). No place type draws backpackers.
  • He can also come in the weekly horde, among the specials.

Loot

Every body has the regular pocket: 45% chance of cloth rags, 0 to 2 things from the pocket list and a 6% chance of a stamp (see Bodies).

On top of that, the trade loot. Each row is rolled separately; the quantity, when there is one, goes from 1 up to the maximum, with the same chance for each number.

This outfit set only drops from this type of zombie (never from place loot). Store it in the radio vault wardrobe to be able to wear it; a duplicate turns into 5 stamps.
