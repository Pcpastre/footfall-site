Every night your base suffers a night attack: a group of zombies pounds on the walls, doors and windows. The attack happens even with the app closed and is resolved when you come back. The base panel warns: "Every night (1 AM) the base gets attacked. Sleeping inside it helps defend."

When it happens

  • Each night is resolved at 1 AM (phone time).
  • With the app closed, missed nights are resolved when the game runs again: at most 3 nights are applied; any beyond that are skipped.
  • New base: the count starts the following night.
  • It only happens if the base has at least one wall built or just marked out. A base made only of building walls is not attacked.
  • Group base: each night counts only once for everyone; the server picks one phone to apply it and the result reaches the others.

How many attack

Regular attack:

attackers = 2 + random roll from 0 to (2 + M) + 3 if the campfire is lit

where M = meters of built wall ÷ 10, rounded. Building walls don't count (they don't fall). Then:

  • +2 if it is night and the floodlight is on (the light draws them).

The campfire and floodlight are checked at the moment the attack is resolved.

Traps first

Before any blow lands, each stake and each covered pit with uses left catches up to a third of the attackers not yet caught (integer division), spending one use per zombie. Caught zombies don't strike.

Damage per blow

Each remaining attacker lands one blow:

damage = (8 + random roll from 0 to 12) × warned × defended

Where each blow lands

  1. If there is any door or window that is closed and intact, there is a 35% chance the blow goes to one of them (picked at random). A window takes 30% more.
  2. Otherwise, the blow goes to a random built wall (building walls never take blows). A wall already knocked down can also be picked.
  3. With no built wall at all, the blow is lost.

Walls and openings never go below 0. At 0 health, the wall falls and the door or window becomes a hole.

Chest losses

After the blows, the game counts the open points:

open = knocked-down walls + walls only marked out + open doors + broken doors and windows

If there is at least one open point, for each item type in the chests (except clothes):

  • 20% chance the type is hit;
  • if it is, half of that item disappears (rounded up).

A base that is closed at the end of the attack loses nothing. The notice sums it all up, for example: "Night attack on the base: 7 zombie(s) (you defended), 2 in the traps. 1 wall(s) down/door(s) open: 3 item type(s) vanished from the chests!" or "... The walls held."

Door open at 1 AMLeaving the door open counts as a wall on the ground: even if no wall falls, the chests are exposed. Close the doors before ending the session.

The weekly horde passing the base

Every week a horde of 30 to 60 zombies crosses your region in a straight line (Wednesday to Sunday, between 7 PM and 10 PM, for 2 h). When the pack passes the point on the route closest to your base:

  • if the route passes more than 300 m from the center of the base, nothing happens;
  • if it passes within 300 m, the base suffers an attack with n = horde size × (1 − distance ÷ 400), rounded, at least 6 and at most the horde size.

This attack follows the same rules as the night attack (traps, damage per blow, multipliers, defense and losses), with two differences: the number of attackers is the horde's (the campfire doesn't add) and a lit floodlight still adds +2. Each horde hits the base only once (on a group base, once for everyone). The notice starts with "The horde passed through the base: ...".

Example: a horde of 50 with the route 100 m from the center of the base: 50 × (1 − 100/400) = 37.5 → 38 attackers.

How to defend

  • Close the base: build all marked-out walls and close the doors.
  • Reinforce the walls (sheet metal 200, concrete 350) and nail planks over the windows (40 → 100). See Walls, doors and windows.
  • Re-armed stakes and pits each catch up to a third of the attackers.
  • Cans and the tower each reduce damage by 15%.
  • Being at the base with the session open at 1 AM cuts the damage in half.
  • Put out the campfire at night (or use the wood stove): a lit campfire brings 3 more.
  • Smaller base = less exposed wall = fewer attackers.
  • Repair the walls every day: 1 plank + 1 nail restore 50 + 5 per Carpentry level.
