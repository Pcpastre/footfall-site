Some large places on the map can be taken over: you beat the defenders in an arena and the place becomes yours (or your group's). A stronghold pays taxes in stamps every day, and you protect it by leaving armed guards. All of this requires an online account ("Strongholds need an online account.").

On the map, stronghold buildings stand a bit taller (at least 9 m) and are colored by owner: free (bandits), another group's or yours. The list of nearby owners is updated every 2 minutes.

How to take a stronghold

  1. Get to the place and open its loot (the loot button on the map).
  2. On the place's screen the flag option appears: Take the stronghold (bandits) for a free stronghold, or Take the stronghold (N guards) for another group's stronghold.
  3. Tap it. The action screen opens in the stronghold's arena and you disappear from the map.
  4. Take down all the defenders without dying, without fleeing and without leaving the arena.

You can't go in with a zombie chasing you ("There's a zombie after you."), with a zombie 15 m away or closer ("A zombie is too close.") or during another fight. A stronghold that was just taken stays protected for a while ("Stronghold just taken: protected for a while."). A stronghold with an owner but no guards is taken on the spot, with no fight.

The arena

The arena is always the same for each place: an intersection of two streets with up to 12 abandoned cars and up to 18 barricades scattered within a radius of about 36 m, surrounded by a ring of barricades with gaps 44 m from the center. There's no place to enter and no furniture to rummage: just the fight. You start 26 m from the center; the defenders appear on the other side, facing you.

Result

  • You won: "(Place) is now yours! Leave armed guards and collect the taxes (4 stamps per day)." (in a group: "your group's"). The action screen stays open: you can walk around and enter the place. You earn 10 Melee weapons XP per defender.
  • You left or fled: "You fell back. N guard(s) still left at (place)." The guards you took down at another group's stronghold stay down.
  • Weapons from downed guards may come to you: "Picked up ...".

Bandits (free stronghold)

Every stronghold without an owner is defended by armed bandits, always the same ones at that place:

  • there are 3 or 4;
  • the first has a 70% chance of having a firearm or bow;
  • the others (and the first, in the other 30%) have a melee weapon.

Taxes

A stronghold of yours yields 4 stamps per day (every 24 h), stacking up to 7 days at most. The guards get paid wages: 12 stamps per week, deducted when you collect.

received = 4 × days − ⌊days × 12 ÷ 7⌋, with days from 1 to 7.

Collect every dayBecause of how the wages round, collecting every day yields 3 stamps per day, versus about 2.3 per day (16 in 7 days) collecting once a week.

Losing a stronghold

  • Another player enters your stronghold's arena and takes down all your guards: the stronghold becomes theirs.
  • Each guard that falls during an attempt disappears with their weapon, even if the attacker backs off afterwards.
  • A stronghold without guards is taken by anyone, with no fight.
  • Right after being taken, the stronghold stays protected for a while.
