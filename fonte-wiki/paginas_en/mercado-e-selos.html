The stamp is Footfall's currency. It isn't an item that weighs on your backpack: it's a wallet balance. With an online account, the server keeps the balance; your phone collects what you earned and spent and sends it from time to time (each operation only counts once). The balance appears on the BACKPACK tab (Wallet: N stamp(s)) and at the top of the mailbox.

Every Stamp item that goes into the backpack, the chests or the depot becomes balance instantly. Stamps from loot go through the server's thermostat: the quantity is multiplied by a factor between 0.6 and 1.4 (it starts at 1), with randomized rounding. The server also caps the wallet's accepted earnings at 1,500 stamps per day.

The mailbox

The market and general store are at the mailbox, in two places:

  • the one in the radio depot (MAILBOX: MARKET AND STORE), which you can reach from any outpost;
  • a mailbox placed at your base (recipe M13: 2 planks + 2 nails).

Away from them: "Use the mailbox in the radio vault (from any outpost) or build one at your base."

Market

The market is a blind order book: "6 slots. Your offer is matched with someone else's at the price of whoever came first, but nobody sees what others are trading: only the guide price."

How to post

  1. In a free slot, tap Sell or Buy.
  2. Find item: choose the item (the list shows the guide and, when selling, how many you have).
  3. Choose the quantity (All or Max fills in the maximum) and the price per unit. The −5%, Guide and +5% buttons adjust the price.
  4. Selling: the form shows "Receive up to N stamp(s) in your wallet (5% fee). Posting: 1 stamp.". Buying: "Sets aside N stamp(s) now (includes 1 for posting)...".
  5. Tap Put up for sale or Place order.

How offers are matched

  • A sale is matched with other people's buy orders priced equal or higher, from highest to lowest; a buy order, with sales priced equal or lower, from cheapest to most expensive. On a tie: the oldest first.
  • The deal always goes through at the price of the offer that was already there. If you ask to buy at 10 and there was a sale at 8, you pay 8 and the 2 difference comes back.
  • An offer can close only partially: "Traded N of M right away; the rest stays waiting."
  • With an open offer, the game checks every 2 minutes and lets you know: "Mailbox: sold 3x Planks (offer complete)."

Withdrawing

Each slot shows "Selling/Buying Nx Item at P stamp(s)" and its progress. Withdraw takes what has arrived: stamps go straight to the wallet and items to the backpack (you need to be at a mailbox). Cancel closes the offer; Free up slot empties a finished slot.

The price guide

Each item has a reference price, measured by how long it takes to find the item while playing (e.g.: Planks 1, Sheet metal 60, 5.56 rifle 212). The guide starts from that price and moves slowly with the market:

  • once a week, the server looks at each day's median over the last 7 days (only days with 3 or more different buyers and 3 or more different sellers; trades within the same group don't count);
  • if 5 or more days were more than 10% above or below the guide, it moves up to 10% in the market's direction;
  • the guide never leaves the range of 0.5× to 2× the reference price.

The thermostat is weekly too: with the market very expensive for 2 weeks, loot stamps drop 5% and the fee goes up 0.5 points; very cheap for 2 weeks, the opposite.

General store

The General store tab works offline: "It sells the basics (stock refills daily; the price rises as it runs out) and buys anything cheap (selling lots of the same item today pays less)."

Selling at the counter

  • The counter buys up to 30 items per day (all items combined).
  • It pays about 1/3 of the guide (rounded, at least 1); items with a guide below 2 pay 0.
  • Each unit of the same item sold on the same day pays 20% less than the previous one (× 0.8 per unit).
  • The button shows how much the next unit pays (+N) and the list shows the guide.

Example: a sheet of metal with a guide of 60 pays 20, the second 16, the third 12 (rounded down).

Value

The Value tab adds up the backpack by the guide ("Your backpack is worth ~N stamp(s) at guide price.") and lists each item with its total.
