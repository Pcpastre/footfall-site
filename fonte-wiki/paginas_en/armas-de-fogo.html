Firearms kill from a distance, but they make noise: every shot wakes up the whole scene and, after the fight, draws the map's zombies from up to 150 m away. Bows and crossbows are the silent exception. Everything here applies to the action screen.

Ammo always goes into your pockets first (see Backpack and weight). Every shotgun shot leaves an empty shell: 4 empties + 1 gunpowder + 2 nails become 4 shells at the workbench (Mechanics 1). Gunpowder comes from a firework.

Reloading

  • The weapon starts every fight with a full magazine (if you have ammo).
  • When you fire the whole magazine and still have ammo, the reload starts on its own ("reloading...").
  • Reload time = weapon reload × (1 - 0.05 × Firearms level).
  • If that weapon's ammo is only at the bottom of the backpack (none in your pockets), the reload takes twice as long ("ammo at the bottom of the backpack...").
  • Switching weapons cancels the reload.
  • Out of ammo: "no ammo" (bow: "no arrows"; shotgun: "no shells").

Aim and spread

Each shot goes in the direction you're facing, with a random deviation within the spread (in degrees, to each side):

Spread = max(1, weapon spread - 0.5 × Firearms) + 0.04 × panic + 6 when running + 2 when aiming at the head + stress/medication - 1 when crouching + 3 on the follow-up shots of a burst. Never below 0.5°.

  • Very high stress: +2; beta blocker: -1.5; cachaça: +2.5.
  • The shot stops at the first wall and hits the first zombie in line (it doesn't go through).
  • Burst (submachine gun and 5.56 rifle): one tap fires 3 shots in a row at the weapon's fire rate. In Automatic mode the weapon fires one shot at a time.
  • In Automatic mode, the character only shoots at zombies it can see within 14 m.

Shot damage

  1. Weapon damage × a random 0.85 to 1.15.
  2. Strong panic: ×0.85; extreme panic: ×0.75. Goes away at Firearms 6 or higher.
  3. To the head: ×2.5 × the zombie's head protection ("HEADSHOT!").
  4. Aiming at the legs (standing zombie): ×0.6 plus leg damage (the leg can break and it crawls; see Melee combat).
  5. To the body: × the zombie's torso protection (vest).

Shotguns

Each shot releases the pellets in a fan the size of the spread (18° on the shotgun, 30° on the sawed-off), plus a deviation of the whole fan of max(1, 4 - 0.4 × Firearms) + 0.03 × panic + 5 when running + stress/medication degrees.

  • Each pellet hits the first zombie in its line. Pellets that hit the same zombie add up.
  • Damage to the zombie = damage per pellet × pellets that hit × distance falloff × 0.85 to 1.15.
  • Falloff: up to 4 m, full damage; after that the damage is multiplied by 1 - (distance - 4) ÷ range, never below 30%. On the shotgun (12 m), 67% is left at 8 m; on the sawed-off (8 m), 75% is left at 6 m.
  • Headshot chance: 6% per pellet that hit, ×1.6 damage.
  • Five or more pellets on the same zombie: "DIRECT HIT!".
  • Shotgun blasts ignore the uniform's protection.
  • Strong and extreme panic reduce damage the same way as with other weapons.

Example: shotgun, 7 pellets point-blank into a zombie at 3 m: 6 × 7 = 42 damage (before the roll), enough for any common zombie (12 to 20 health).

Silencer

With improvised silencers in your backpack, each one muffles 6 shots of 9 mm ammo. A muffled shot only wakes those within 30 m in the scene and doesn't count as noise on the map after the fight. Every 6 muffled shots, one silencer falls apart ("The silencer fell apart."). It's made at the workbench: 1 bottle, 2 rags and 2 duct tapes (Mechanics).

Bows, crossbows and arrows

  • Only 5 m of noise when firing, and an arrow that hits doesn't wake the zombie it hits or its neighbors.
  • Magazine of 1: each arrow is a reload (bow 0.6 s, crossbow 2.2 s).
  • After the fight you recover 60% of the arrows fired, rounded down ("Picked up N arrow(s).").
  • Arrows: 1 plank, 5 nails and 1 duct tape make 5 at the workbench (Carpentry).

Noise after the fight

Any loud shot (no silencer, not .22) draws every zombie on the map within 150 m of where the fight took place. With only .22, within 52.5 m. See Noise.

Flashlight on the weapon

The "flashlight attached" modification (1 flashlight, 1 duct tape, 1 wire) increases action-screen vision at night by 35%.
