In Footfall you play by actually walking. Everything that happens on the map (zombies moving, looting, distance traveled, noise) only runs while a session is open. Outside a session the character is "sleeping" or "standing still" where you ended, and only real time passes (hunger, thirst, fatigue, healing).

Starting a session

  1. Have a character created. Without one, the map shows Create character and starting is refused with "Create a character before you start.".
  2. Tap START SESSION at the bottom of the map.
  3. The game checks whether the device's location is on ("GPS off. Turn on your device's location.") and whether permission was granted ("Location permission denied.").
  4. It asks for your current position for up to 12 s. If the GPS doesn't respond, it uses the last known position and corrects it later. Without either, "Couldn't get your GPS position. Go somewhere open and tap again." appears.
  5. The whole attempt has a 30 s limit. Past that: "The GPS took too long to respond. Go somewhere open and tap again.". Tapping twice doesn't open two sessions.

When it starts, the game:

  • applies the rest from the time the app was closed (see Sleeping or ending);
  • resets the session distance (it's used for the rare item chance, see Looting);
  • checks the month (zombies come back every month, see Zombies), the night attacks on the base and the weekly horde;
  • schedules the first random alarm for 8 to 11 minutes from now;
  • loads the streets and places around you (first 500 m, to start quickly, then 1,400 m) and fetches the real weather.

The map is downloaded within a 1,400 m radius. When you get more than 550 m from the center of the downloaded chunk (and enter another chunk), the game downloads the new chunk on its own. If the internet fails, it tries again after 5, 15, 30, 60, 120 and then every 300 s; the notice at the top shows the countdown and tapping it retries right away.

Delivery driver professionIn the first session, the delivery driver already starts with about 400 m around them marked on the explored map ("You know this neighborhood well: it's already on your map.").

The start message in the log is "Session started. You can be attacked.": from here on the zombies on the map can see, hear and chase you.

Sleeping or ending

To finish, open the Menu (three-dot icon in the left corner) and choose:

  • Sleep (end here), when you're somewhere safe: inside the base, at an outpost or at the tent camp.
  • End session, anywhere else ("Outside the base: poor rest, without losing anything.").

A window confirms (Sleep / End or Keep playing). Ending anywhere keeps everything you're carrying. The difference is in the rest while the app stays closed:

The menu's Home screen button is locked during the session ("End the session first.").

Compass and calibration

You only spot zombies in a cone in front of you (see Vision), so where your character is facing matters. There are two ways to turn:

Compass on

  • The top of the phone is in charge: the reading uses the accelerometer and magnetometer with tilt compensation.
  • The direction snaps to 12 fixed positions, every 30 degrees. To switch positions the reading has to go past the halfway point with 5 degrees of slack, so it doesn't jitter.
  • The compass counts as "alive" if it sent a reading in the last 2 s; otherwise the game acts as if it were off.
  • Automatic calibration: when walking between 0.7 and 3.5 m/s, every 4 m the game compares the direction you're walking with the reading and corrects 10% of the difference.
  • Calibrate using the street (Settings → Calibrate using the street, only with the session open): stand on a street, point the top of the phone along it and tap. There must be a street within 25 m and the reading must be no more than 60 degrees off the street's direction.
  • Fine tuning: a slider from -45 to +45 degrees added to the reading.

Compass off

  • Every 4 m walked, the character faces where you're walking.
  • The amber stick turns your gaze instantly (up = north). After you move it, the gaze stays locked for 6 s before movement turns the character again.

What counts as distance

The session distance goes up with each GPS reading, but only when all of these conditions hold:

  • you're not on the action screen or on the confirmation screen before it ("Walking with this screen or the action screen open doesn't count distance");
  • the game hasn't flagged you as a vehicle (see below);
  • GPS accuracy is 30 m or better;
  • the estimated speed is greater than 0.4 m/s;
  • the jump between two readings is less than 80 m (bigger jumps are GPS errors and are ignored).

The GPS only sends a new position when you walk at least 3 m. Speed is the average between the previous and the new one (the GPS's, when it reports it; otherwise, the distance over the last few seconds). With no new reading for 3 s, the speed halves every half second.

The same distance also gives Stamina experience (1 point every 40 m running above 3 m/s, 1 every 150 m walking) and Stealth experience (1 every 15 m walking below 1.8 m/s with an awake zombie less than 40 m away).

Above 7 m/s for 3 s in a row, the game flags you as a vehicle: "Too fast! Maximum noise, distance doesn't count." appears, noise goes to the max and nothing you travel counts (neither distance nor experience). The flag clears as soon as your speed drops below 7 m/s.

Standing still

  • Standing still (below 0.4 m/s) you rest a little from fatigue while the session runs.
  • Noise stays at the minimum (0.01).
  • Some things require standing still: looking through binoculars (up to 0.8 m/s and without moving 4 m from the spot), pitching the tent (1 minute) and looting (moving 20 m from the point where you started cancels; see Looting).
  • With no bleeding or infection, and hunger and thirst below level 2, health rises 1 point per minute with the session open.

Pause on avenues

Crossing an avenue while looking at your phone is dangerous, so the game pauses the threat when you're on a major road. This applies to primary, secondary and trunk roads (and their links), which the game treats as 12 m wide: if the nearest street is within 8 m and you're less than 7 m from its centerline (half the width + 1 m), the banner "Cross carefully — threat paused" appears.

While it's on screen:

  • zombies on the map stop completely (they don't move, don't notice you, don't chase);
  • the action screen doesn't start on its own.

The fight button still works if you want to open the action screen yourself.

No internet

The game is online. Without a connection the session is paused: nothing moves, nothing counts and the GPS is ignored until the connection comes back.
