The radio vault is a personal room, yours alone, that every outpost and your tent lead to. It holds your 300 kg inventory, a workbench, the wardrobe, a mailbox, the shelf of memories and the radio that takes you to the base. It belongs to the account: it stays yours if you leave a group and when the character dies.

How to get there

  1. Go to one of your outposts or to your pitched tent (within 25 m of them).
  2. The Outpost: radio vault button (or Tent: radio depot) appears on the map, and it glows when you can go.
  3. Tap it: the action screen opens inside the vault room. You disappear from the map while you're there.

You can't go if you're in a fight ("Finish the fight first."), with a zombie chasing you ("There's a zombie after you.") or with a zombie 10 m away or closer ("A zombie is too close.").

A 50 m² square (7.07 m per side). Get within 1.3 m of each thing and tap the button that appears:

Rent is charged at the turn of the week (Monday). Each week pays the highest cost it had: emptying the vault or taking down outposts before the turn doesn't get you out of that week. Weeks without opening the game pay the current cost (up to 8 weeks at once). Without enough stamps, you pay what you have: "Vault and outpost rent: paid X of Y stamp(s) (short)." Charging starts the week after the first one.

The vault can go over 300 kg without you meaning to when you join a group and your base is disbanded: everything goes there.

The crow and the vault

With enough bond, the crow carries light things from your backpack to the vault (from level 2) and brings you one thing from the vault (level 4).

Outposts

An outpost is a point on the map that only you can see. While at it (within 25 m), you can go to the radio vault and, from there, to the base. Ending the session at an outpost counts as sleeping (full rest, like at the base, but without the bed bonus).

  1. During the session, go to where you want the outpost.
  2. Base → BASE → Set up outpost here (free) or Set up outpost here (uses 1 radio). Another way: use the Outpost radio in the backpack.

The list shows each outpost with its distance ("Outpost 2 · 1.4 km from you") and the Dismantle button: the radio goes back to the backpack; the free one can be set up again.

The Outpost radio is extremely rare: the first character of each account and device gets one (along with 10 stamps); otherwise, it drops with a 0.3% chance per place searched inside, or in the rescue flare (medium) crate for whoever lit it. Having an outpost that isn't the free one also counts as having a radio.

Playing from homeThe game recommends not building your base where you live. Set up the free outpost at home: nobody can see outposts, and from it you go to the base through the action screen.

From the outpost to the base

In the vault, RADIO: GO TO BASE opens the action screen inside the base (at its center, or at the nearest point inside). Through the radio you can only be at the base and in the yard; to go back, use the action screen's exit button. Without a base: "You don't have a base yet." (in a group without a base: "The group doesn't have a base yet."). Through the base, you can't drop items on the ground.

Tent

The tent is a portable outpost: once pitched, it also leads to the radio vault and the base, and ending the session near it counts as a proper sleep.
