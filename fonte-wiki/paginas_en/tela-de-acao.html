The action screen is where fighting happens: a top-down scene built from the real streets, buildings, cars and furniture around you, plus every zombie in range. On it you move with the joystick (not with GPS), fight, search places from the inside and search bodies.

When it starts

On its own

If a zombie that is chasing you gets within 25 m of you on the map, the action screen opens on its own. This doesn't happen:

  • during a grace period (see below);
  • inside the base with the walls standing (closed base);
  • during the avenue pause ("Cross carefully — threat paused", see Session and map).

With the button

The big fight button on the map opens the action screen immediately, anywhere. Anyone who hasn't seen you starts unaware: you can sneak up from behind.

Other ways in

  • Enter a place from the loot screen (see Looting).
  • Go to the base through an outpost or the tent.
  • The radio vault and the arena of a held point also use the action screen.

Confirmation: "THEY'RE HERE"

When the fight started on its own, before the scene a warning screen appears with the number of zombies in range: "Before fighting, stop somewhere safe: on the sidewalk, away from the street, not crossing anything. The fight will wait for you." The fight doesn't advance while it's open. Tap I'M STILL AND SAFE · FIGHT to start.

With this screen or the action screen open, walking in real life doesn't count distance (neither for rares nor for missions).

The scene starts with you facing where you were facing on the map. If the GPS put you inside a building, you start on the nearest street.

At the start, notes on what weighs on this fight appear for a few seconds: hunger, thirst, fatigue, pain, fracture, illness, load, night, fog, rain, stress, medicine.

Free roam

The regular action screen, opened on the street (by button or on its own), is free roam:

  • past 40 m from the center, the scene restarts around you: the terrain is rebuilt, new zombies in range come in (up to 40 in the scene) and those left far away leave;
  • you can only step where your explored map already covers (the rest stays dark), at the base and in its yard;
  • getting within 3.5 m of a place's door opens it from the inside right there; leaving and moving 6 m from the door closes it again;
  • there's no escape edge.

In the other scenes (place opened with Enter, base through the outpost) there's a red edge at 120 m: crossing it is escaping.

Places without fighting

  • Closed base: inside the base with the walls standing, map zombies stop chasing you and forget the noise they heard, and fights don't start on their own. The streets inside it are blocked to them.
  • Avenues: during the crossing pause, zombies stop completely.
  • Radio vault and arena: no map zombie gets in.
  • Bunker safe rooms: the armored door doesn't let zombies through (see Bunkers).

Leaving

The regular action screen doesn't end on its own when everyone dies: you leave when you want. There are three ways:

Door icon

In the top right corner, the exit icon has a green border when you can leave. It only works if:

  • no living zombie is within 10 m ("Zombie within 10 m: move away to leave.");
  • no one is chasing you ("N zombie(s) chasing you: lose them (stay 40 m from them for 3 s), kill them or escape through the edge.");
  • you're not on the ground ("Get up first.") or grabbed ("Break free first.").

Losing them

If all zombies that know about you stay more than 40 m away for 3 s straight, "gave it the slip!" appears. On the regular action screen they just lose sight of you (going back to unaware) and then the door icon unlocks. In a scene that ends on its own (the arena), losing them counts as escaping.

Red edge

In scenes with an edge, going beyond 120 m from the center ends the fight as an escape ("You escaped").

Inside a place

In a place opened with Enter, walking out through the door (after having stepped inside) takes you straight back to the street.

At the end the summary appears (You died, You escaped or Horde defeated, with kills, health and time) and Back to map.

What the fight carries over to the map

  • Health, panic, ammo, throwables, weapon in hand and durability pass straight to the character (the backpack is updated live during the fight).
  • Wounds become injuries on the body. A bite causes stress and sadness.
  • Arrows: 60% of those shot come back. Each shotgun shot leaves an empty shell.
  • The killed become a pile of bodies on the map (except in bunkers and the arena) and don't come back until the month turns.
  • Gunshots echo: see Noise.
  • Experience: each hit gives 4 in Melee weapons (6 in Firearms with a firearm; with fists, 4 in Strength) and 1 in Stamina; each kill gives 6 in Strength and each shove 2.
  • Health at 0: "Killed by the horde.".
