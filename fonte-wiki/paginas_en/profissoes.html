The profession is the first choice in character creation. It sets how many points you have to spend on traits, gives starting levels in skills and, in some cases, free traits or an effect of its own. The profession doesn't change later: it lasts until the character dies.

How to read the points

Positive points are left over to buy positive traits. Negative points are a debt: you need to take enough negative traits for the balance to reach 0 or more before starting. The better the profession, the more negative it is.

A profession's free traits come already checked, cost no points and can't be removed. They also block the traits that conflict with them (for example, a Personal trainer can't take Asthmatic, because they already have Athletic).

The skill levels given by the profession add up with those from traits such as Handyman and First responder, capped at level 10.

Professions, one by one

Unemployed

+8 points. No skills or traits. It's the profession with the most free points: you can build the character with traits alone, for example Athletic (8) or Strong (8) without taking any negatives, or combine several mid-cost traits. Good for those who already know which traits they want.

Police officer

-4 points. Firearms 3 and Melee weapons 1. Starts out shooting well: Firearms level 3 already takes 1.5° off the shot spread, reloads 15% faster and lands more headshots (see Skills). Needs 4 points in negative traits. Pairs well with the .38 revolver from the starting kit.

Firefighter

0 points. Melee weapons 1, and for free Fit (+20% max stamina) and Sturdy (+15% melee damage). That's 8 points of traits at no cost. Since they already have Fit and Sturdy, they can't take Athletic, Out of shape, Sedentary, Strong, Weak or Scrawny. Good for those who will fight in melee from day one.

Nurse

+2 points. First aid 2 and Light-footed for free (-30% noise when walking and running). At level 2, each treatment step takes 16% less time, sutures fail 24 percentage points less (from 35% to 11%) and wounds heal 10% faster. Can't take Clumsy.

Doctor

-2 points. First aid 4. It's the best profession for caring for wounds: treatment steps 32% faster, sutures and shard removal with no chance of failure when you are calm (35% − 48% and 25% − 48% reach 0), healing 20% faster and items that restore health yielding 40% more.

Carpenter

+2 points. Carpentry 3. Already meets the minimum level for every carpentry recipe (the most demanding one, the Watchtower, requires 2), repairs more wall health per plank (50 + 15) and almost never cuts themselves on tools (3% − 1.5% = 1.5% per recipe).

Mechanic

+2 points. Mechanics 3. Unlocks right away the recipes that require Mechanics 1 (spear, workbench repair, floodlight, shell reloading) and each weapon repair yields 15% more.

Farmer

+2 points. Farming 3. The garden grows 15% faster (72 h becomes 61.2 h) and each harvest gives 4 vegetables instead of 3.

Military veteran

-8 points. Firearms 2, Melee weapons 1 and Desensitized for free: immune to panic, no stress and sadness from wounds and fights, and no phobias. It's the most expensive profession: it requires 8 points of negative traits. Since they have Desensitized, they can't take Brave, Cowardly, Hemophobic, Claustrophobic or Agoraphobic.

Personal trainer

-6 points. Athletic for free (+40% max stamina, an 8-point trait). Can't take Fit, Out of shape, Sedentary or Asthmatic.

Night watchman

+2 points. Melee weapons 1 and Night vision for free: at night, vision on the map doesn't shrink (120 m instead of 40 m) and on the action screen you can see up to 26 m without a flashlight, instead of 12 m. Great for those who play after dark.

Delivery driver

+2 points. Wanderer for free (+10% to the distance counted for rare loot). On top of that, they "know the city": in the first session the explored map already starts with about 400 m around you. Can't take Homebody.

Cook

+2 points. Skilled cook for free: all food that isn't spoiled yields 25% more calories (see Needs). It doesn't change the water that food gives.

Burglar

-2 points. Light-footed for free and 50% faster looting: the bar for opening each piece of furniture or container moves 1.5 times as fast (a 6 s piece of furniture takes 4 s). Less time standing still opening things means less time for zombies to find you. Can't take Clumsy.

Your profession is gone when you dieWhen you die you lose the profession, the traits and the levels. The new character chooses everything again. See Death.
