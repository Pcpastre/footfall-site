In Footfall you don't see the whole map: you only spot zombies inside a cone in front of you, up to a distance that changes with the time of day, the weather, the flashlight and your traits. Walls block vision and dense woods shorten it. Zombies also have a cone, a smaller one, and only see you inside it. This page explains both sides.

Your cone on the map

A zombie appears on the map when any of these is true:

  • it is within 6 m of you, in any direction (sound and smell). With binoculars this radius drops to 2 m;
  • or it is inside the cone, within the current range, and no wall blocks the line to it.

Panic narrows the cone on the map: 0.25 degree less per point of panic (up to 25 degrees at panic 100). This doesn't apply to binoculars, which always stay at 28°.

Range multipliers

After the base value from the table, the range is multiplied by everything that applies (in sequence):

Watchtower: base with a placed tower and you less than 40 m from the base×1.5
Scout role in the group×1.05
Rain×0.75
Fog×0.6
Infection in the delirium stage×0.6

Example: at night, with a flashlight, in the rain: 90 × 0.75 = 67.5 m in a 60° cone.

Walls and woods

  • The game casts 360 rays from you. Each ray stops at the first building wall. The building you're in right now is ignored (GPS sometimes puts you inside a house).
  • Dense woods (the map's vegetation areas) don't stop vision, but each meter inside them costs 4 m of range. With 120 m of range, 30 m of woods is enough to end vision in that direction.
  • What you see gets marked on the explored map (the Map panel).

Jump scare

A zombie that suddenly appears less than 15 m away adds up to 6 points of panic (more the closer it is). With vibration on, the phone gives a buzz when a new zombie enters your vision.

Flashlight

You need a flashlight in the backpack, or a helmet with a light being worn (miner helmet, tactical helmet or headlamp), and batteries.

  • The button only appears at night, and the flashlight turns itself off when day breaks.
  • It uses 1 battery every 20 min it's on (30 min with a headlamp or helmet). Without batteries it goes out ("The batteries ran out.").
  • It changes your cone to 60° and 90 m (see the table above).
  • It gives you away: with the flashlight on, zombies see you at 30 m instead of 8 m. On top of that, every 3 s, every zombie within 50 m with a clear line to you (and that isn't lying down or already chasing you) wakes up and heads to where you are.
  • On the action screen, a handheld flashlight that's only at the bottom of the backpack has to be dug out first (2.5 s; see Backpack and weight).

Binoculars

With binoculars in the backpack, the eye button on the map turns on long-distance vision. It only works while standing still (up to 0.8 m/s: "Stop to look through the binoculars."). While looking:

  • the cone becomes 28° and the range goes up to 300 m by day or 140 m at night (with Night vision, 300 m at night too), before the multipliers;
  • what's nearby disappears: the 6 m "around you" radius drops to 2 m;
  • turn your phone (or the stick) toward where you want to look: streets, places and zombies in the cone get marked on the map.

Moving faster than 0.8 m/s, getting 4 m away from where you started or taking the binoculars out of the backpack puts them away ("Put the binoculars away.").

Vision on the action screen

On the action screen vision is calculated differently, in scene meters:

SituationRange
Day30 m (×1.3 Eagle eyes, ×0.7 Nearsighted)
Night or a dark bunker basement12 m
Night with Night vision26 m
Night with flashlight22 m (this applies even if you have Night vision: with the flashlight on it's 22, not 26)
Fog×0.6 on the result
Flashlight mounted on the weapon in hand (modification), at night×1.35

At night the values also take the Eagle eyes or Nearsighted multiplier and use the lower of the night value and the day value. The final result always stays between 8 and 40 m.

The cone width on the action screen is 160° (180° with Eagle eyes), 20° less with a closed helmet (motorcycle helmet or riot helmet). Panic takes off 0.5 degree per point and having less than 15 stamina takes off another 15°, but the cone never closes below 110°. Walls, tall furniture and cars block the rays; a zombie right up against you within 0.9 m is always sensed.

Zombies that leave the cone fade out gradually (0.8 s; 1.6 s if you're Nearsighted). Those behind you and out of sight show up as sound markers around your character, within a 6 m radius (10 m with Keen hearing, 3 m with Bad hearing, and 30% less with a closed helmet).

How zombies see you on the map

Each zombie has a 100° cone in the direction it's facing. It only sees you if you're inside it, within range and with no wall in between.

SituationZombie vision range
Day15 m
Night8 m
Full moon night10.4 m (8 × 1.3)
Night, with your flashlight on30 m

Multipliers on that range:

  • Wearing a Dark sweatshirt, at night: ×0.85.
  • Rain: ×0.75.
  • Stealth skill: 4% less per level.
  • A dormant zombie (not yet noticing anything): sees and hears at half.
  • A zombie lying down: doesn't see; only hears you right up close (30% of the hearing radius).

Seeing isn't instant: the zombie gradually notices you. Right up close, it takes about 1 s; at the edge of its range, 2 s. If you leave its sight, it gradually forgets. Once it finishes noticing, it starts chasing you, and so does every zombie within 20 m of it (whoever saw you wakes up the neighbors; this doesn't happen when it only heard you). For sound, see Noise.

Crouching (action screen)

There's no crouching on the map. On the action screen, the CROUCH button changes how unaware zombies perceive you:

  • a standing unaware zombie only sees you within ±50° of where it's looking, with a clear line, up to 7 m; crouched, only up to 4.5 m;
  • the speed at which it notices you drops by half when you're crouched (and is still multiplied by your footstep noise: traits, Stealth, running shoes and sweatpants);
  • a zombie lying down and unaware only notices you within 1.2 m;
  • crouched you move at 45% speed and can't run.

When running, any unaware zombie within 5 m (times the footstep multiplier) notices you instantly. More details in Controls.
