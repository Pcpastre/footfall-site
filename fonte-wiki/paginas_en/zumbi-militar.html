A soldier who died trying to contain the outbreak. Helmet and vest: he is the most armored of the uniformed zombies and has almost double the health. He owns the bunkers.

Behavior

On the map he is like any zombie: he can be dormant, wandering or lying down, sees in a 100° cone and hears your noise (see Zombies). The uniform only changes the fight and the loot.

He is 5% slower than a common zombie, but makes up for it with protection and health.

Where it appears

  • Near bunkers and inside them: 50% + 12% per floor of the bunker's zombies are soldiers (85% at most). The briefing officer and the one carrying the vault card are also soldiers. See Bunkers.
  • In the weekly horde: 6% are soldiers, on top of the usual specials.
  • On the street: 18% of street specials.
  • Waves from the hard flare (25%).

Loot

Every body has the common pocket: a 45% chance of cloth rags, 0 to 2 things from the pocket list and a 6% chance of a stamp (see Bodies).

On top of that, the job's loot. Each row is rolled separately; the quantity, when there is one, goes from 1 up to the max, with the same chance for each number.
