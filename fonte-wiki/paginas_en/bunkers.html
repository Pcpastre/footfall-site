Every month, a few squares in the city hide a military bunker. On top it's a building like any other; below, a staircase leads to basement floors that get ever more dangerous, darker and full of dead soldiers, all the way to the vault at the bottom. The bunker is yours alone: no one else sees what you took from inside.

Where they appear

  • Each square on the map has a 10% chance of becoming a bunker for the month. The roll is per square and per month: everyone sees the same bunkers.
  • On the map it shows as "Military bunker (square name)", place type Military bunker.
  • Bunkers change at the start of each month.

Floors

Each bunker has 3 to 6 floors in total, the same for everyone: the ground floor (rolled like a regular building, with the entrance furniture) and 2 to 5 designed basements. The radio in other bunkers shows this total.

Basement themes follow depth: the first is always the Garrison and the last is always the Vault. In between come, without repeats and in this order, up to 3 of Logistics, Command, Armory and Laboratory.

Each floor plan has 4 mirrored versions, picked by the bunker.

On the stairs: GO DOWN (basement N), GO UP (basement N) and, on the first, UP (entrance).

Basement zombies

  • Per floor: 6 + 2 per floor below the first + 0 to 2 at random, spread across the room area (never in the safe room).
  • Plus the plan's specials: 3 locked in the quarantine cells, the ones on the range, the officer in the briefing room, the Brute in the antechamber.
  • Each bunker zombie has a chance of being a soldier of 50% + 12% per floor, at most 85%. The rest are regular.
  • 15% of them are lying down (in the barracks, 50%).
  • The zombies come back every time you leave and re-enter the bunker. Each one's body only yields loot once per month.

Safe rooms and return point

Every basement starts in a safe room: the stairs up, an armored door (you get through, zombies don't get through or break it), the FLOOR PLAN on the wall and a supply locker with one simple thing (water, bandage, batteries, crackers, gauze or painkiller).

  • The plan shows the floor's rooms and doors, with no zombies or loot.
  • Stepping into a safe room saves your return point there. If the app closes in the middle of the visit, entering the bunker again takes you straight to the last safe room ("Back in the safe room of basement N.").
  • Leaving through the front door clears the return point: the next visit starts from the ground floor. Dying inside also clears it.

Darkness and the generator

The basements are as dark as night: vision on the action screen drops to 12 m (22 m with a flashlight, 26 m with Night vision). Bring a flashlight and batteries.

Floors with an engine room (Logistics and Vault) have a generator: START THE GENERATOR (biodiesel).

  • It uses 1 can of biodiesel ("You need a can of biodiesel (make it at the base fire: oil, alcohol and lye).").
  • The whole floor lights up until you leave the bunker.
  • The engine makes 80 m of noise and every zombie on the floor heads to the generator.

Biodiesel is made at the base fire: 2 cooking oil, 2 alcohol 70% and 1 lye.

Quarantine

In the Laboratory, the quarantine has cells behind bars, with 3 zombies locked in. The CELL PANEL raises all the bars at once ("Clang! The cell bars went up... all of them."), with 30 m of noise. The cells stay open until you leave the bunker.

Bunker radio

On the Command floor, the BUNKER RADIO lists the month's other bunkers within a 5 km radius, with distance, direction and number of floors. With none: "Just static: no other bunker within 5 km."

The vault door

On the last floor, the door between the antechamber and the vault is a vault door. Near it you get:

Where the card is

  • If the bunker has a Command floor: in the small safe in the commander's room, which is locked (crowbar, 3 s).
  • If it doesn't: an undead officer on the second-to-last floor carries the card on the body ("Body: Officer").
  • The card is only valid in the month it was found: at the turn of the month it vanishes from the backpack and the vault.

The bunker's rarity multiplier grows with depth: 1.5 + 0.8 per floor below the ground floor (see the rarity roll).

In addition:

  • One guaranteed rare on each basement, except the last (the vault one), in any piece of furniture on the floor. The ground floor has none.
  • Magazine: each ammo box has 1 or 2 calibers in boxes (6 to 15 rounds each) and a 12% chance of a rare.
  • Morgue drawer: a body's pocket (and a 25% chance that someone gets up).
  • Bunker safe: 2 extra rares and, sometimes, a special item: hard flare (15%), tent (10%) or medium flare (20%). Lucky and Unlucky change these chances by 10%.

Bunker rares come from the rare list of the military bunker: ballistic vest, tactical vest, ballistic helmet, tactical helmet, rifle, hunting rifle, submachine gun, pistol, shotgun, antibiotic, morphine, 5.56 and .308 ammo, canned food, radio, tactical backpack, tactical pants, forearm guard and tarp.

Monthly reset

  • Once looted, the bunker stays marked until the start of the next month (it doesn't restock weekly like other places).
  • In the new month the bunkers move to other squares, the map's zombies come back ("New month: the zombies are back.") and old access cards vanish.
  • A running generator and open cells only last for the current visit.
