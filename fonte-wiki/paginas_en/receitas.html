Recipes are in Backpack → CRAFT. Each one shows the ingredients, the tools and what it requires (workbench, lit fire, skill level) and a Make button. When something is missing, the recipe gives the reason (e.g.: "Missing Planks (1/4).", "Needs Carpentry 2.", "Missing tool: Hammer.").

General rules

  • Ingredients are used up. They come from what's loose in the backpack (what's worn doesn't count), from the base chests (if you're at the base) and from the radio vault (if you're in it).
  • Tools aren't used up. They count if they're in the backpack or in your hand.
  • Workbench: you have to be using a workbench from the action screen (USE WORKBENCH). The base workbench, the one in the radio vault and the one in the basement of the bunkers all count.
  • Fire: only at the base's lit fire (campfire or stove), by tapping COOK. See Cooking.
  • Structure (furniture): only inside the base or in the yard. The furniture comes out "stored" and you place it with the hammer (see Base furniture).
  • Experience: only recipes with a skill give XP, and only if you have the minimum level. Rebuilding a piece of furniture you dismantled gives no XP. Reading the skill's book gives +50% XP for 1 day.
  • Accident: Carpentry or Mechanics recipes have a 3% − 0.5% per skill level chance of cutting your hand deeply.

Both repair the weapon in your hand ("Hold a weapon to repair it."). How much comes back:

durability restored = total × base × 0.8repairs × (1 + 0.05 × Mechanics level), at least 1, without going over the total.

  • base = 0.5 for R06 and 1.0 for R08;
  • repairs = how many times that weapon has already been patched; each repair adds 1 ("Weapon repaired (35/40). Patched 2 time(s).").

Example: a crowbar (70 swings) repaired for the 3rd time with R08 and Mechanics 4 gets 70 × 1.0 × 0.64 × 1.2 = 53.8 → 54 durability. The repair count follows the weapon when you swap and store it (storing doesn't repair).

Order on screen

The list follows the game's order. When you open the tab from the fire (COOK), fire recipes come first. The text above the list says where you are: "At the workbench: uses what's in your backpack and in the chests.", "On the fire: cooking goes further and the food lasts again." or "No workbench: make one and place it inside the base. Some recipes don't need it."
