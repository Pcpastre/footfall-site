Traits are characteristics chosen during character creation. Positive ones cost points; negative ones give points back. The final balance (profession points minus the cost of the traits) must be 0 or more. Conflicting traits can't be chosen together, and the rule works both ways: if A conflicts with B, checking A disables B.

There are 28 positive traits and 23 negative ones. Traits last until death; you can't gain or lose traits during play.

What each trait does, in detail

Stamina: Athletic, Fit, Out of shape, Sedentary, Asthmatic

Max stamina starts at 100, loses 0.4 per point of fatigue and other penalties (fever, pain, cold...), and only then is multiplied by the trait: Athletic ×1.4, Fit ×1.2, Out of shape ×0.8, Sedentary ×0.6. The Stamina skill adds +3% per level to the same multiplier. The result always stays between 30 and 150. Asthmatic doesn't touch the maximum: it multiplies by 0.5 the rate at which stamina comes back during a fight.

Damage: Strong, Sturdy, Weak, Scrawny, Baseball player

A beginner's melee damage starts at 75%. Strong adds 30 points, Sturdy 15, Weak subtracts 30 and Scrawny subtracts 15. Melee weapons adds 5 per level and Strength 3 per level (see Skills). Baseball player multiplies the result by 1.15 when the weapon in hand is the Baseball bat or the Nail bat, besides giving +1 in Melee weapons. Strong and Weak also change the weight limit by ±20% (pockets and backpack).

Noise: Light-footed, Hunter, Clumsy

Footstep noise is multiplied by 1 − 0.3 (Light-footed) − 0.15 (Hunter) + 0.3 (Clumsy) − 0.04 per Stealth level, with a minimum of 0.3. Light-footed and Hunter can stack. Clumsy also multiplies by 1.5 the noise of opening furniture and containers when looting. The same multiplier applies on the action screen, where it determines how long zombies take to notice you.

Vision: Eagle eyes, Nearsighted, Night vision

  • Eagle eyes: vision range ×1.3 on the map and on the action screen; on the action screen the vision cone opens to 180° instead of 160°.
  • Nearsighted: range ×0.7; on the action screen zombies that leave your vision fade from the screen more slowly (1.6 s instead of 0.8 s).
  • Night vision: at night, vision on the map stays at 120 m (without the trait it drops to 40 m, or 60 m on a full-moon night, or 90 m with a flashlight). On the action screen, at night and without a flashlight, you see up to 26 m instead of 12 m.

Hearing: Keen hearing, Bad hearing

On the map, the game plays the groan of the nearest zombie that's chasing you or is within 25 m, from its direction. Without the trait, this works up to 40 m; with Keen hearing, up to 70 m; with Bad hearing the alert doesn't exist. On the action screen, the radius in which you notice zombies out of sight is 6 m (10 m with Keen hearing, 3 m with Bad hearing), and a closed helmet multiplies that radius by 0.7.

Fast and Slow reflexes

They affect the action screen: the critical hit window becomes 15% larger (or smaller); in Automatic mode the interval between automatic swings is ×0.9 (or ×1.1); and the zombies' attack wind-up seems slower (×1.12) or faster (×0.9), giving more or less time to dodge.

Thick skin and Thin skin

When a hit is about to become a wound, Thick skin cancels the wound 30% of the time (only the damage remains, halved). Thin skin does the opposite: when clothing stops the hit, 30% of the time the game rolls the same attack again, with the same clothing, and it may get through. A bite stopped by clothing becomes a bruise and isn't rolled again.

Fast healer and Slow healer

They multiply by 0.7 (or 1.3) the healing time of every treated wound. They combine with the −5% per First aid level and with the base stretcher (×0.67).

Strong immunity and Weak immunity

They change the chance that a zombie wound turns into zombie infection:

Note that the real numbers don't exactly match the trait texts: Strong immunity also halves the chance for lacerations and scratches, and Weak immunity multiplies those by 1.6.

Organized and Disorganized

They multiply by 1.3 (or 0.7) the capacity of the pockets and the worn backpack, together with Strong/Weak (the bonuses add up: Strong + Organized = ×1.5).

Lucky and Unlucky

They multiply by 1.1 (or 0.9) the chance of a rare item when looting. On the action screen, Lucky also adds 3% to the critical chance and to the chance that a melee weapon doesn't lose durability on a swing; Unlucky subtracts 3% from the critical chance.

Light eater, Big eater, Low thirst, High thirst

They multiply by 0.75 (or 1.25) all calorie or water consumption: the daily amount and that of each kilometer walked. See Needs.

Panic: Brave, Cowardly, Desensitized, Adrenaline junkie

How much panic rises is multiplied by 0.3 (Brave), 2 (Cowardly) or 0 (Desensitized). Desensitized also zeroes the stress and sadness caused by fights and wounds and cancels the phobias. Adrenaline junkie makes you 10% faster on the action screen in Strong panic and 25% in Extreme panic.

Experience: Fast learner, Slow learner, Leader

Fast learner multiplies all XP by 1.3; Slow learner by 0.7. Leader changes the group role bonus from ×1.25 to ×1.5. See Skills.

Starting skill: Handyman, First responder, Baseball player, Hunter, Gardener

They give +1 level in the indicated skill, added to the profession's (cap 10). E.g.: a Doctor with First responder starts with First aid 5.

Skilled cook

Unspoiled food gives 25% more calories. Eating at the base table raises the yield another 10% (×1.25 × 1.1). The food's water doesn't change.

Wanderer

The chance of a rare item grows with the distance walked in the session (1% standing still, +1.5% per km, up to 15%). Wanderer counts that distance as 10% greater, and the same goes for the chance of finding outfit sets.

The trickiest traits

Smoker

Smoker gives back only 2 points, but requires a Pack of cigarettes every 4 real hours. The clock starts at character creation: 4 h after being created, if you haven't smoked yet, withdrawal kicks in (moodle Craving a smoke). While it lasts:

  • max stamina ×0.9;
  • stress rises 0.03 per second with the app open (about 1.8 per minute);
  • every fight starts with 10 more panic.

Smoking (Smoke button on the item) resets the clock, removes 20 panic and 25 stress ("Smoked a cigarette. Calmer."). Non-smokers can't smoke. The clock also runs with the app closed, so anyone away for a long time comes back already craving a smoke.

Homebody

It only applies if you have a base. More than 2 km from the nearest stretch of base, max stamina is multiplied by 0.8. Without a base, the trait does nothing. Outposts and the tent don't count as a base for this rule.

Claustrophobic and Agoraphobic

The phobias create a panic floor in certain places:

  • Claustrophobic: when there are 4 or more buildings within 15 m of you.
  • Agoraphobic: when the nearest street (within 15 m) is wide, 9 m or more: avenues and the map's main and secondary roads (types primary, secondary, trunk, tertiary and their ramps).

The floor is 35. On the map, panic doesn't drop below 17.5 (half the floor) while you're in these places; on the action screen it rises on its own to 35 (Panicked). Since panic narrows vision, reduces damage and worsens suturing, the phobia weighs heavily. Desensitized cancels both, and they can't be chosen together.

Hemophobic

Seeing blood causes panic:

  • +10 panic for each treatment step done on the Health screen;
  • +35 panic on a quick dressing;
  • +12 panic (multiplied by your panic gain, at least ×0.5) each time you're wounded on the action screen.

Conflicts with First responder and Desensitized. Since panic at level 2 or higher increases the chance of the suture failing, a hemophobic character can fall into a vicious cycle when treating themselves right after a fight.
