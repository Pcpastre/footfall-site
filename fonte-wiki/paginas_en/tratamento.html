Every wound is treated step by step on the Health tab of the character panel. Each wound shows only the steps still missing, in the right order to do them. It only heals once all steps are done and there's no infection. Treating gives First aid XP, and the skill level makes each step faster and safer.

Where to treat

  • On the map: open the character panel (heart icon, Character) and go to the Health tab. The Vitruvian figure at the top shows the state of each body part; tapping a part highlights it. Below are the bars (Health, Hunger, Thirst, Fatigue, Stress, Sadness, Boredom, Pain) and one card per wound.
  • On the action screen: the same panel opens straight to the Health tab when you're hurt. You can also treat yourself in the middle of a fight.
  • On the base stretcher: tapping the stretcher on the action screen opens Health and reminds you that wounds heal faster there.
When you can't treatOn the map, no step can be done with a zombie chasing you: "There's a zombie after you. Get away first.". On the action screen, the block is any zombie within 6 m: "A zombie is too close. Move a few meters away to treat yourself.".

How a step works

  1. On the wound card, each missing step is a button with the step's name and, below it, the item that will be used (or in hand, or missing <item> if you have none; then the button is disabled).
  2. Tapping it opens a window with the step's name, the item used, warnings ("It is at the bottom of the backpack: finding it takes longer.", "Chance to fail: N% (level and panic count).") and a bar with the seconds remaining.
  3. You can tap Stop to give up before the bar fills; nothing is used up.
  4. When the bar fills, the item is used up and the step is done (or fails). The result appears, for example "Bandage (with Gauze): done.".

The item is looked for in the backpack and, if you're at the base, in the chests. On the action screen, the game prefers what's in your pockets; if the item is only at the bottom of the backpack, the step takes 2.5 s longer.

Details of each step

  • Clean and disinfect: marks the wound as clean. With cachaça, the Empty bottle is left over.
  • Remove the shrapnel: with tweezers, the tweezers aren't used up. Without tweezers, it's done by hand: the step is still possible ("No tweezers: done by hand (it hurts and bleeds).") and takes 3 health.
  • Suture: uses 1 suture kit per attempt. Closes the deep cut and the hemorrhage; on a hemorrhage, it also releases the tourniquet.
  • Bandage: with a Sterile dressing or a First aid kit, the wound also counts as clean. With a rag, the dressing starts out dirty. If you have Medical tape, it's used up along with it and the dressing takes 24 h instead of 12 h to get dirty. When the dressing gets dirty, the button comes back as Change dressing.
  • Tourniquet: stops the hemorrhage instantly. Don't go more than 2 h without a suture: after that it takes 1 HP every 120 s.
  • Immobilize (splint): the only step for a fracture. The splint is made with 1 plank and 2 rags.
  • Ointment: first step for a burn; bandage it afterwards.

When it fails, the item has already been used up (the suture kit is gone; the tweezers aren't), you lose 3 health and gain 2 XP. The messages are "Your hand shook and the suture opened (-3 health). Try again." and "The shard slipped (-3 health). Try again.". No step drops your health below 1.

Calm down before stitchingPanic at level 2 or higher weighs heavily on sutures. Move away, wait for the panic to drop or take a Beta blocker (panic rises less) before suturing. Cachaça clears panic instantly. See Mind.

Quick dressing

When any wound is bleeding, the Health tab shows the Quick dressing (<item>) button (or No bandage in the backpack, disabled). It uses the first one you have, in the backpack or, at the base, in the chests: Bandage, Gauze, Sterile dressing, First aid kit. You can also use these items straight from the inventory (Use).

  • It's instant and bandages the wound that bleeds the most (not counting hemorrhages).
  • If the only bleeding wound is a hemorrhage, the dressing becomes a tourniquet on it.
  • It doesn't clean the wound, not even with a sterile dressing: "Applied a quick dressing. An uncleaned wound can get infected: check the Health screen.".
  • It uses medical tape if you have any.
  • It gives 15 First aid XP (Hemophobic: +35 panic).
  • The item also restores health (see below).

Use the quick dressing to stop the bleeding in a pinch and treat it properly afterwards.

Healing runs in real time, including with the app closed. The stretcher (base furniture, "Stretcher" recipe, requires Carpentry 1) only counts while you're inside the base with the session active; when you open the app after time away, the time that passed is counted without it.

First aid book

Reading the First aid book gives +50% First aid XP for 1 real day and removes 20 boredom.
