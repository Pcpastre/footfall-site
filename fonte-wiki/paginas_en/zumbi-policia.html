A police officer who died in uniform. The vest stops half the damage to the body and it takes 30% more punishment. It carries ammo and, sometimes, its service weapon.

Behavior

On the map it is like any zombie: it can be dormant, wandering or downed, sees in a 100° cone and hears your noise (see Zombies). The uniform only changes the fight and the loot.

Where it appears

  • Near police stations: of the 25% chance that a zombie within 50 m is job-specific, 2 in 3 are police officers.
  • On the street: 12% of street specials (which are 7% of zombies).
  • Inside police stations (same rule, by place type).
  • Waves from the easy flare (8%) and the medium one (15%).

Loot

Every body has the common pocket: 45% chance of cloth rags, 0 to 2 things from the pocket list and a 6% chance of a stamp (see Bodies).

On top of that, the job-specific loot. Each row is rolled separately; the quantity, when there is one, goes from 1 to the maximum, with the same chance for each number.
