In Footfall there are two different things called "clothes":

  • Actual clothes (clothing-type items): you wear them in a body slot, they protect against bites, scratches, blows and gunshots, keep you warm, weigh on you in the heat, give pockets and wear out until they tear.
  • Outfits (your character's look): Outfit: ... items that you store in the wardrobe at the radio depot. They only change your appearance and stay with you even after you die.

Body slots

Each piece of clothing goes in one slot. New clothing in an empty slot is put on automatically (except in slots you emptied on purpose).

How protection works

Each piece of clothing has two parts: how much of each body part it covers (e.g., the jacket covers 88% of the arms; the rest are the gaps at the cuffs and collar) and the material, which stops each type of attack in its own way.

MaterialBiteScratchBlowShotWear per bite blocked
Fabric10%30%10%0%25
Denim/canvas35%60%20%0%15
Leather85%90%35%5%8
Rigid95%95%70%30%4
Ballistic95%95%60%80%5

A piece's protection = coverage × material resistance × condition, where condition goes from 1 (new) to 0.5 (torn) depending on wear. Pieces in layers on the same body part stack: whatever gets through one still has to get through the other (total protection = 1 − the product of what each one lets through).

Example: arms with a sweatshirt (9% against bites) and a forearm guard (66%) block 1 − 0.91 × 0.34 = 69% of bites to the arm.

With no clothes at all, you have 4 kg of pockets (hands and shallow pockets). Pockets and backpack are multiplied by the character's capacity (traits). See Backpack and weight.

Extra effects

  • Running shoes: footsteps 20% quieter. Sweatpants: footsteps 8% quieter. The two together multiply.
  • Motorcycle helmet and Riot helmet are full-face: they cut side vision and a bit of hearing.
  • Miner helmet, Tactical helmet with light and Headlamp: the light points where you look and batteries last 50% longer (with batteries in any pocket).
  • Raincoat and Windbreaker: in the rain you don't feel its cold.
  • Cap: removes 1 from the heat level during the day.

Cold and heat

The feels-like temperature is the real temperature minus 4 °C in the rain (without a raincoat or windbreaker) and minus 2 °C at night. At the base, or with hot soup in effect (3 h), you don't feel cold.

Wear and torn clothes

Each piece of clothing has wear from 0 to 100%. It wears out in two ways:

  • When it blocks an attack: each piece covering that body part wears by material wear × attack factor × coverage. Factor: bite 1.0, blow 0.7, scratch 0.5, shot 1.2. Example: a bite blocked on the torso wears the sweatshirt by 25 × 1.0 × 0.9 = 22.5%.
  • When a hit lands on a body part, the clothes covering it also tear a little.

Each Tailoring level reduces all wear by 5%.

At 100% the clothing tears: "... tore: half protection until you sew it.". Torn, it:

  • protects only half (condition drops linearly from 1 new to 0.5 torn; a piece at 50% wear protects 75%);
  • carries half in its pockets;
  • doesn't keep you warm.

With any torn clothing, the Torn clothes notice appears.

Sewing

On the BACKPACK tab, each piece of clothing with 10% or more wear appears with its condition ("uses 35%" or "torn (half protection)") and the Sew button.

Outfits (your character's look)

Your character's look on the action screen is an outfit. You start with the Survivor. You find the others as items (Outfit: ..., 1.2 kg) or earn them by finishing memories. No one sees your name above your character: the outfit and the colors (skin, hair, shirt, in Settings → Appearance) are what tell one player from another.

The wardrobe

  1. Bring the Outfit: ... item to the radio depot (through an outpost or the tent).
  2. Get close to the wardrobe and tap WARDROBE: STORE NEW OUTFIT (or WARDROBE). Outfits in the backpack go in automatically.
  3. Tap WEAR on the outfit. You can only change outfits there ("You can only change outfits at the radio vault wardrobe.").

Stays after deathOutfits stored in the wardrobe belong to the account, not the character: when you die, they're still there. An outfit that was in your backpack (not yet stored) goes to the pile where you fell, like any item.

How outfits appear

  • In loot inside places: each item rolled from interior furniture (not the entrance's) has a chance of being an outfit: 0.12% × (1 + meters walked in the session ÷ 2,000) × 1.5, capped at 0.6% (traits that change distance count; luck traits don't). Walking more in the session raises the chance. At the outfit's "right" place, it weighs 4 times more in the roll.
  • On the job-specific zombie's body ("more dead"): each uniformed zombie has a 10% chance of carrying the full outfit.
  • In rescue flare crates: the medium one has a 5% chance and the hard one 10% of bringing a random outfit.
  • In memories: finishing a memory unlocks its outfit straight into the wardrobe.
