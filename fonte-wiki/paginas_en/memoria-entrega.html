The last delivery is the game's first memory and works as a tutorial. Unlike the others, it is not a book: the game offers it the first time you log in, right after you create your first character, and it is always playable on the shelf at the radio depot.

How to start

On your first login, the THE LAST DELIVERY screen invites you: PLAY THE FIRST NIGHT or GO STRAIGHT TO THE CITY (skip; the game won't ask again). Afterwards, play it from the shelf at the radio depot. Playing from the shelf ends your session, because the memory uses a different character.

Gameplay tips

  • The flashlight is in the left corner of the screen. It helps you see, but they see you too.
  • Floors are connected by stairs: get close and use GO UP / GO DOWN.
  • Those who haven't seen you yet have a cone on the ground: come up from behind, crouched, for the stealth kill.
  • Rummaging through furniture takes time and makes noise. Stay still until the bar fills.
  • A zombie raising its arm or opening its mouth is about to attack: get out of reach. If grabbed, run in the opposite direction.
  • If you fall too early, choose FROM HERE to continue from where you were.
  • The record is for kills: it's worth coming back to beat it.
