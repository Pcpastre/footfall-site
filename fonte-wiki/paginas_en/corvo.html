The crow is a companion only you can see. It follows you on the map, warns you about hordes, points out bodies, shows you one place with something rare each day and, once bonded, carries things to and from the radio depot. The Crow button appears on the map during the session after it arrives.

Arrival

The crow arrives when you put the first door on your base (as in Poe's poem, it perches above the door): "A crow landed on your door. It stays." From then on it's yours. See Walls, doors and windows.

What it does on the map

The crow flies at 9 m/s on the map. Every 4 seconds it looks around, in this order of priority:

Group messages

When a group mate sends a quick alert (HORDE HERE, LOOT HERE, HELP!), the crow comes flying from your mate's direction (from up to 150 m away) and caws when it arrives.

How much bond each food gives

bond gained = food value × 0.6times it has already eaten today × (0.5 if hunger is below 25)

  • The first meal of the day counts in full, the second 60%, the third 36%, the fourth 21.6%...
  • When it's barely hungry (below 25), it counts for half.
  • With hunger below 5 it doesn't eat: "Its crop is full. Try later."
  • Each meal takes 45 off its hunger.
TipOne piece of fresh meat a day, while it's hungry, gives much more than several foods on the same day. Raw meat spoils in 1 day, but it's fine for the crow.

Taking things to the depot (level 2+)

  1. On the crow's sheet, under Take to the radio depot, tap the items (each tap adds 1; going over the weight removes the item). Only items that fit within its weight on their own appear.
  2. Tap Send the crow.

The items leave your backpack. It flies to the base at 11.1 m/s (about 40 km/h): the sheet shows "from here to base: ~N min flight (one way)". When it arrives, the items go into the depot; then it comes back to you ("The crow is back: delivered to the radio vault."). The flight counts with the app closed too.

Fetching from the depot (level 4+)

Under Fetch from the depot, tap an item that's light enough. It flies to the base, takes the item out of the depot and brings it to your backpack ("The crow came back with ..."). If the item left the depot while it was flying: "The crow came back empty...".

Distracting (level 5)

Caw and lure up to 3 zombies: it swoops down cawing and pulls up to the 3 nearest zombies (within 60 m, that are chasing you, suspicious or within 30 m) toward a point 45 m from them, on the side opposite you. They stop chasing you and go after it for about 20 seconds. After that it's tired for 10 minutes ("It's still tired (N min)."). It doesn't work at night.

Flight rules

  • It needs a base ("The crow lives at the base: without one, it has nowhere to fly.") and an open session.
  • The crow doesn't fly at night. A flight that runs into the night lands and finishes the next morning.
  • When hungry (70+), it doesn't fly.
  • While it's flying, it disappears from the map and doesn't warn about hordes, zombies or rare places.

The crow's memory

There's a playable memory of it, unlocked after the other six. See The crow.
