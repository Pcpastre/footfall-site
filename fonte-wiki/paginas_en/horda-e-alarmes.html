Besides the zombies that live on each street, the world has events: a horde that crosses the area every week, alarms that go off and pull in the zombies around them, supply drops left by helicopter and rescue flares, which you light to face waves and earn a crate. The horde and the drops are the same for every player in the same area.

The area is a square of about 2 km (0.02 degree). Your "location" for events is your base; without a base, wherever you are.

Radio warning

With a radio (in the backpack, in a chest, in the vault, or at an outpost with a radio), Radio Crow announces: "Horde of about N coming from the (direction): (day) at HH:MM." and, if you have a base, whether it passes close ("Passes about X m from your base." when the route is less than 300 m away; otherwise "The route is far from your base."). When it starts moving: "The horde is moving now. Stay away from its route or defend the base."

The horde on the map

  • The pack's position appears on the map if you have a radio or if you're less than 400 m from it.
  • When the pack passes less than 500 m from you, its zombies actually appear around the point (within 35 m), already awake and walking toward where the pack will be 3 minutes later.
  • They follow the pack; if they hear or see you, they leave it and come after you like any zombie.
  • Uniforms: 6% soldiers, plus 7% street specials (see Zombies).
  • When the horde ends, those of its members that aren't chasing you disappear.

Damage to the base

If the route passes less than 300 m from the center of the base, when the pack goes by the closest point the base suffers a horde attack (once per horde, applying to the whole group at the group's base):

  • Attackers = horde size × (1 - distance ÷ 400), between 6 and the total size. At 0 m they all come; at 200 m, half.
  • Traps catch some first: each armed stake or pit catches up to a third of the remaining attackers, using up charges.
  • Each attacker takes 8 to 20 health off a wall or opening. 35% of the blows go to doors and windows (windows take 30% more).
  • With a warning, the damage is lower: tin can alarm ×0.85, watchtower ×0.85, floodlight lit at night ×0.8. If you're at the base with the session open, ×0.5.
  • If any wall fell or any door was left open, each type of item in the chests (except clothing) has a 20% chance of losing half.

The summary appears as "The horde passed through the base: N zombie(s)...".

Rescue flares

The flares (easy, medium, hard) are the challenge you choose: light it, hold off the waves of zombies and, if you win, a helicopter drops a rescue crate.

Lighting

  • Use the flare in the backpack with the session open.
  • Only on the street: not inside a place, not in the vault, not at the base ("Light the flare in the street, not inside a place.", "Light the flare outside your base.").
  • One flare at a time ("There's already a flare lit nearby. Finish that one first.").
  • Anyone within 800 m gets the alert and can come help.

  • The first wave arrives 2 min after lighting.
  • Between one wave and the next there's a 90 s breather ("Wave N cleared! The next one comes in 90 s: heal and reload.").
  • Each helper within 150 m of the flare when the wave starts increases the wave by 40%.
  • Each wave comes from a different side (the direction appears in the alert) and spawns 30-45 m from you (or from the flare, if you're less than 60 m from it).
  • Flare zombies know where you are: they come in a straight line, never lose your trail and never give up because of distance.
  • The next wave only starts when everyone from the current wave is dead.
  • Timers only run with the session open. A flare left idle for 24 h with nobody playing goes out.

The top of the map shows the status: "Flare (level): 1st wave in N s", "Wave N/T: X zombie(s)", "Wave N/T cleared · next in N s".

The rescue crate

  • Once the last wave is beaten, the crate drops: "Helicopter! The rescue crate dropped at the signal: 30 min to grab what you can."
  • You need to be within 100 m of the flare when it drops.
  • Those who helped also get a crate, as long as they took down at least one zombie from the last wave.
  • Each participant has their own crate, which stays for 30 min. Opening takes 4 s.
  • The crate's ammo is for the weapon you have in hand (with no firearm, 9 mm).

Where to find flares

  • Inside places, one chance per place: easy 1.2%, medium 0.4%, hard only after 3 km walked in the session (0.2% to 0.8%). See Looting.
  • In the bunkers' safe: hard 15%, medium 20%.
  • In the rescue crates themselves (easy gives medium, medium gives hard, 3% each).
Prepare the spotLight it in an open area, with ammo in your pockets and dressings at hand: the waves come from different sides and the breather is only 90 s.
