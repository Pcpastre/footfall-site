On the action screen each swing goes out in an arc in front of you, up to the weapon's reach, and hits one or more zombies. The damage depends on the weapon, your character, where you aimed, panic and stamina. This page has all the math. For the buttons, see Controls.

Melee weapons

Reach is measured from the edge of the character to the tip of the weapon. Stamina per swing is 6 + 3 × the weapon's weight (before the tiredness and pain multipliers).

How a swing lands

  • The swing hits the zombies inside the weapon's arc and reach, from nearest to farthest, up to the weapon's number of targets.
  • A low zombie (downed or crawling) is reached with 20 extra degrees on each side, and one right up against you (less than 0.5 m) is always reached.
  • With no zombie in the arc: "missed" (stamina is spent anyway).
  • Each hit pushes the zombie back a little and makes 4 m of noise around it.

The damage math

For each zombie hit:

  1. Base: weapon damage (+3 with the "blade on the tip" modification).
  2. × your damage multiplier: 0.75 base, +0.30 Strong, +0.15 Sturdy, -0.30 Weak, -0.15 Scrawny, +0.05 per Melee weapons level and +0.03 per Strength level. The Baseball player hits 15% harder with bats. Strong (-10%) or extreme (-20%) hunger, fever or nausea (-10%), delirium (-20%), pain (-5% per level) and depression (-10%) also come in here.
  3. × a random 0.85 to 1.15.
  4. × 0.85 if the swing was automatic.
  5. Panic: panicked (level 2) -10%, strong (level 3) -20%, extreme (level 4) -30%.
  6. Missed head: when aiming at the head of a standing zombie, there's a chance to hit the shoulder (see below): the swing becomes a body hit at ×0.7.
  7. Not aiming (Two sticks mode with the right stick released): ×0.85, and a 25% chance of a glancing blow (×0.5) if it isn't a critical.
  8. Critical: ×1.6.
  9. Out of stamina (below 15): ×0.6.
  10. Zombie lying down: ×1.5 (finishing blow).
  11. Body part (only on a standing zombie): see the table.

A beginner (level 0, no traits) hits for 75% of the weapon's damage; with Melee weapons 5 it reaches 100%.

Criticals

A critical only comes from the tip of the weapon: the zombie has to be in the last band of the reach (or be downed/crawling, which always counts).

  • The band starts at reach × (0.8 - bonus + 0.15 × panic ÷ 100), between 50% and 97% of the reach. The bonus is +0.15 with Fast reflexes, -0.15 with Slow reflexes, and +0.02 per Melee weapons level. On screen, it's the yellow band.
  • Manual swing (tap) in the band: guaranteed critical if you're aiming (fighting stance or right stick), otherwise a 35% chance (+3% Lucky, -3% Unlucky). In any case, each panic level takes off 5% (last-instant flinch).
  • Automatic swing: 4% + 0.2 × bonus + luck - 1.3% per panic level, without needing the band.
  • A critical deals ×1.6 damage, pushes hard and makes the zombie stagger (0.4 s, 30% chance to fall), unless it's already on the ground or crawling.
  • A zombie hit by a critical (or already downed) with less than 45% health has a 35% chance of never getting up again and starting to crawl.

Recovery is multiplied by your pace: Asthmatic ×0.5, +5% per Stamina level, and less when very thirsty (-20%), dehydrated (-40%), bleeding (-15%) and stressed.

Max stamina is 100 minus 0.4 × tiredness, minus fever (-25), infected wound (-15), heavy pain (-10), cold and heat (-8 per level) and delirium (-35), times the traits (Athletic +40%, Fit +20%, Out of shape -20%, Sedentary -40%, +3% per Stamina level), Smoker without a cigarette (-10%), Homebody more than 2 km from the base (-20%) and nausea (-15%). It stays between 30 and 150.

Out of stamina

  • Below 15: swings 50% slower, damage ×0.6 and the vision cone narrows by 15 degrees.
  • Below 10: you walk at 70% speed.
  • Below 5: you can't run.

Swing speed

The weapon's interval is multiplied by: 1.5 when out of stamina; 0.9 with the "wrapped grip" modification; and by attack slowness (exhausted +10%, at the limit +20%, heavy load +15%, pain +7% per level, morphine +10%, anti-anxiety pills +8%).

Shove

  • Tap the left half of the screen. 0.9 s interval.
  • Costs 20 - Strength in stamina (minimum 12).
  • Hits every zombie within 1.3 m in front (±100°; in Automatic mode, all around).
  • Throws the zombie back at (3.2 + 0.1 × Strength) m/s, minus 8% per panic level, for 0.55 s.
  • Chance to knock down: 45% + 4% per Strength level, at most 80%, minus 10% per panic level.
  • Frees you from whoever is grabbing you.
  • Takes off 10 panic points.
  • On the ground, the shove spends stamina but does nothing: move the stick to get up.

A knocked-down zombie stays on the ground for 5 s and takes another 1.2 s to get up (without attacking). While it's lying down, hits on it deal ×1.5.

Zombie attacks

Each attack has a visible wind-up (arm rising, mouth opening) before the hit. Get out of reach during the wind-up and it misses ("dodged").

The wind-up is multiplied by the zombie (the inmate winds up 20% faster, the Brute 25% slower) and by your reflexes (Fast reflexes give you 12% more time, Slow reflexes 10% less). After attacking, the zombie waits 1.2 s. Its reach is 0.7 m (0.6 m when crawling), with 0.3 m of leeway at the moment of the hit.

Clothing can stop the attack: then there's no wound and you take only half the damage ("your clothes held"). A stopped bite becomes a bruise. The Thick skin and Thin skin traits change the chance that a hit becomes a wound.

Being knocked down: 5% chance of a fracture (arm or leg) and 35% of a bruise.

Grabs

  • When attacking while standing, a zombie has a 40% chance of trying to grab instead of scratching. At most 2 zombies can grab you at the same time. The Brute never grabs.
  • Grabbed: +10 panic, you move at 25% and lose 5 stamina per second ("grabbed! run the other way").
  • After holding on for 0.7 s, it tries to bite.
  • Breaking free: pull the stick away from whoever is holding you. The force to break free rises with how much you pull in the right direction (×1.5; ×2.2 when running), +8% per Strength level. When it reaches 1: "broke free!".
  • Also freeing you: the shove and any hit that lands on the zombie that's grabbing you.
  • When it lets go, it's pushed half a meter and waits at least 0.8 s before attacking again.

Stealth kill

An unaware zombie (one that doesn't know about you yet) hit from behind by any weapon (fists don't count) dies instantly: "STEALTH KILL". From behind means outside its cone (more than 50 degrees from where it's looking) or with it lying down. A stealth kill makes no noise and wakes no one.

On screen, zombies that haven't seen you yet have a cone drawn on the ground. Crouch to get closer without being noticed (see Vision).

Durability and repairs

  • Each swing that hits at least one zombie spends 1 durability (once per swing, even when hitting several).
  • Chance of not spending it: 3% per Melee weapons level, plus 3% with Lucky.
  • When it reaches 0: "Name broke!". The weapon disappears and you're left with your fists.
  • Putting the weapon away and taking it out again doesn't repair it.
