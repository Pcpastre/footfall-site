Footfall measures hunger and thirst like a real person's consumption: a calorie reserve and a water reserve that go down over time and with every kilometer walked. The Hunger (in kcal) and Thirst (in ml) bars are on the Health tab of the character panel. Tiredness, which is also there, has its own page: Tiredness and sleep.

The effects of hunger and thirst add up: sick with hunger and dehydrated at the same time, you lose 5 HP per minute. Dying this way gives the cause "You succumbed to hunger and thirst.".

From level 2 (Starving or Very thirsty), stress rises by 0.02 per second with the app open.

With the app closed

Outside the session nobody starves: consumption continues, but the reserve stops at 11% (264 kcal and 264 ml), just before the damage level. If you were already below that when you closed the app, the reserve stays where it was. Damage from hunger and thirst only happens with the session active, on the map.

Come back with foodAfter a day or two without playing, you open the app near 11%, already Starving and Very thirsty. Eat and drink before you start walking: below 10% your health starts dropping.

When health comes back

While playing

With the session active, on the map, health comes back at 1 HP per minute as long as all of these conditions hold:

  • no bleeding wound (a deep cut bandaged without a suture counts as bleeding);
  • no wound with a common infection;
  • hunger below Starving (reserve of 25% or more);
  • thirst below Very thirsty (reserve of 25% or more).

The zombie infection doesn't prevent this regeneration. Max health is 100.

While sleeping

With the app closed, health only comes back if you ended the session in a safe place or with a sleeping bag, and you're not bleeding or carrying the zombie infection:

Items

Some medical items give health back instantly; see Treatment.

Eating and drinking

In the inventory, the item's button is Eat or Drink. Calories are multiplied like this (water doesn't change):

SituationCalories
Spoiled food×0.5 (and you get sick)
Skilled cook trait×1.25
Eating at the base table×1.1

Eating beyond the full reserve doesn't store the excess: everything stops at 2,400.
