Looting means opening furniture in real places on the map (pharmacies, markets, police stations, houses...) to find items. Each piece of furniture takes time and makes noise. The more you've walked in the session, and the deeper you go into the place, the better the chance of something rare. What you take is gone for the other survivors until the place restocks.

Where you can loot

The map's Loot button searches within 35 m of you (the GPS margin):

  • places on the map (shops, services; see the types in Health, Market, Restaurant, Construction, Gas station, Police, Fire station, School, Clothes, Electronics, Shop);
  • houses: any building of 20 to 600 m² with no shop, with you within 12 m of the wall;
  • bodies of killed zombies and items dropped on the ground (bodies take priority when everything is close);
  • supply drops and rescue crates (see Horde and alarms);
  • the month's bunkers.

With only one thing nearby, it opens directly. With more than one, the Loot list appears (up to 8, from nearest to farthest), and places already looted show "already looted (you can enter)". With nothing: "Nothing to loot nearby".

You can also go inside by walking on the action screen: getting within 3.5 m of a place's door opens it from the inside on its own; outside free-walk mode the ENTER: place name button appears.

Rummaging

  1. On the loot screen, tap Rummage (X s · noise) on a piece of furniture. On the action screen, get within 1.3 m of it and tap RUMMAGE FURNITURE NAME.
  2. Stay still until the bar fills. One piece of furniture at a time.
  3. Once it's open: tap an item to take it (ammo comes as the whole stack) or tap Take all.

Time = furniture time ÷ (looting speed). The Burglar profession loots 1.5× faster; being sad (sadness level 2 or more) makes it 10% slower.

Interruptions:

  • On the map, moving more than 20 m from the point where you opened the loot cancels it ("Looting interrupted: you moved away.").
  • If the action screen starts (a zombie saw you), the loot closes ("Looting interrupted!") and reopens on its own when the fight ends, if you're still within 35 m.
  • On the action screen, moving more than 0.45 m, being knocked down or being grabbed stops the search ("Stopped rummaging.").

Opening the first piece of furniture marks the place as looted for 7 days (the bunker, until the start of the next month). A looted place can still be opened: whatever's left is still there.

Glass cuts

Each piece of furniture you open (bodies don't count) has a 4% chance of you cutting your hand on a shard: a shard is left in your hand ("You cut your hand on a shard of glass: there's a shard in it. Check the Health screen.").

The rarity roll

Each item comes out like this:

  1. Base rare chance = 1% + 1.5% per km walked in the session, at most 15%. The Wanderer trait counts 10% more distance.
  2. Times luck: Lucky ×1.1, Unlucky ×0.9.
  3. Times the place: through the door ×0 (never rare), inside ×1.5, bunker ×(1.5 + 0.8 per floor below ground level).
  4. Roll: below the rare chance you get a rare; up to 22 percentage points above that you get an uncommon; the rest is common.
  5. It picks an item of that rarity from the place type's list. If there's none of that rarity, it goes down one (rare → uncommon → common).

Legendary items never come from this roll, only from the special finds below. The distance is that of the current session: ending it resets it.

Radio tip

With a radio, once a day the radio points to a place not yet looted between 300 m and 1.2 km away ("Weak signal: there's untouched stock at..."). That place gets 1 extra rare item from its list, once.

Weekly restocking and server stock

  • Each place is built once per week. The stock week is counted in 7-day blocks of the server clock.
  • When logged in, the server stores the week's first build and everyone sees the same stock: what another survivor already took is no longer there. If you both take the same thing at the same time, whoever confirmed later loses the item ("Another survivor took it first: ...").
  • Zombies killed inside, forced doors and dismantled furniture stay that way until the week turns over. Then the place fills up again.
  • Bunkers, rescue crates and memories are yours alone: nothing goes to the server.
  • Without a connection, what you take is queued and confirmed when the network comes back.

Locked doors and the crowbar

Inside, some ground-floor doors are locked. Getting close shows FORCE DOOR:

  • it requires a crowbar, in hand or in the backpack ("Locked. Needs a crowbar (or a zombie inside breaks it).");
  • it takes 3 s standing still and makes 30 m of noise ("Door forced open (noise!).");
  • the door also comes down if a zombie pounds on it until it breaks (100 health; each blow takes 5 to 9). Windows have 40.

An opened door stays open until the week turns over, for everyone. The keys (armory key, pharmacy key) only appear inside memories. The bunker's vault door has its own rules: see Bunkers.

Dismantling furniture

A wooden piece of furniture that's already been rummaged and is empty can be dismantled with DISMANTLE (planks · 8 s · noise). It requires a hammer, crowbar, axe, fire axe or sledgehammer.

  • Furniture: shelf, cabinet, kitchen cabinet, student lockers, counter, drawer, pantry, table, school desk, bookcase, wardrobe, nightstand, bed, back stockroom and vault.
  • It yields 1 to 2 planks (2 to 3 from the big ones: cabinet, wardrobe, bookcase, bed, counter, stockroom and vault) and, with a hammer, 1 to 3 nails.
  • It takes 8 s, makes 30 m of noise and gives 8 Carpentry experience.

Tips

Walk before going inThe rare chance only grows with the session distance. Going into the good places after a few kilometers yields much more than looting at your front door right after opening the app.
Display cases and armories call the streetGlass display cases, gun racks and locked armories make 24 to 30 m of noise. Clear out the zombies nearby first.
