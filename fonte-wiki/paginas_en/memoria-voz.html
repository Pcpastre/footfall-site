The crow's voice is the memory of a radio host at a neighborhood community radio station, on the second day. It teaches you to handle a generator and fuel, light and noise, and to hold a position.

Gameplay tips

  • The building has parts that only connect by stairs: use GO UP / GO DOWN and remember where you came from.
  • The generator and lights make noise and draw attention: before turning them on, know where they can come from.
  • Light helps you see who's coming, but it also attracts. Turn it on and off carefully.
  • The bat is already in hand; the flashlight needs the batteries (which are in your pocket).
  • Holding a position is easier at a narrow chokepoint: they arrive one at a time.
  • If you go down, FROM HERE restarts from the last checkpoint.
