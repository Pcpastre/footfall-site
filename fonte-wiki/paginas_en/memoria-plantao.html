The night shift is the memory of a nurse at a neighborhood emergency room on the first night. It teaches wound treatment step by step and how to choose whom to treat first.

Profile
ProtagonistNurse Lúcia · day 0, small hours
Where to find itThe book Memory: The shift (a shift planner with the name Lúcia on the cover), inside health places, with a 0.6% chance per place
Challenge typeWound treatment (clean, suture, bandage), triage and a locked pharmacy
ProfessionNurse (First aid 2 and the Light-footed trait, free from the profession), no other traits
Starting kitNothing: everything you use is in the emergency room itself
RewardNurse outfit (scrubs, ponytail, badge and scissors in the pocket)
ScoreboardKills

Gameplay tips

  • Treatment is done through the Health screen (the heart): each wound shows its steps, each with a time and a specific item. With First aid 2, each step takes 16% less time.
  • Search the nurses' station and the cabinets: the dressing supplies are scattered around.
  • The special actions (treat, open, lock) appear as buttons when you get close.
  • A locked door needs the right key or another way out: explore before forcing it.
  • Treating someone means standing still for a while: check that no zombie is coming before you start.
  • If you go down, FROM HERE restarts from the last checkpoint.
