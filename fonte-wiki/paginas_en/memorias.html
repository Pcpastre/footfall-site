Memories are short, playable stories of other people's first days. Each one (except the first and the last) is a rare book found inside a specific type of place. Once read, the book goes onto the shelf in the radio vault, from where the memory can be played as many times as you like. The reward is purely cosmetic and a trophy: an outfit set and a record.

In this wiki the memories appear spoiler-free: only where to find them, who the person is, the type of challenge and gameplay tips.

Where the books appear

  • Each book only appears in its own type of place (table above).
  • Only inside: in the furniture of the place's interior (the entrance ones don't count). Quick looting from outside never gives a book.
  • The chance is 0.6% per place, rolled when the place's stock is set up (once a week at each place), and the book goes into a randomly picked piece of interior furniture.
  • It only appears if you haven't read that memory yet and don't have the book in your backpack or in the base chests.
  • The books are legendary and weigh 0.3 kg.

Putting it on the shelf

  1. Take the book and tap Use in the backpack (anywhere).
  2. The book leaves the backpack and the memory goes onto the shelf: "(Title) is on the shelf in the radio vault..."
  3. To play, go to the radio vault (through an outpost or the tent) and tap SHELF OF MEMORIES (N).

Duplicate book: if the memory is already on the shelf, the book turns into 3 stamps ("That book is already on the shelf: it became 3 stamps.").

The shelf

The shelf is in the radio vault, on the left wall, below the wardrobe. It shows one spine per memory: the ones you've read in color with the title, the missing ones dark. The top says how many you have ("N of 7 memories."). Tapping a book opens its sheet:

  • read: the title, who and when, what it teaches and the record;
  • unread: "A rare book, found inside places." (The last delivery: "Finish the tutorial to keep it here."; The crow: "Appears once the other six have been played.").

The PLAY (or PLAY AGAIN) button opens the memory. The last delivery can be played from the shelf even if it was never finished.

Playing a memory

  • Playing ends the session ("The session ends to play (the memory uses another character). Just start again afterwards.").
  • You control the person in the memory, not your character: their profession (with only the profession's free traits), with their outfit and their own kit. Some start with the weapon already in hand.
  • Your character, backpack, base and everything else are kept and come back exactly as they were at the end.
  • While the memory runs, nothing goes to the cloud or the server (save, position, combat, kills). There are no other players.
  • The controls are the ones you chose in Settings.
  • The memory takes place in its own locations, with floors (up and down buttons) and special actions that appear as buttons when you get close.

Failing and restarting

If the memory's character goes down too early, you see "The memory failed — That's not how it ended. Try again?" with three options:

ButtonWhat it does
FROM HERERestarts from the last checkpoint, with full health and no wounds
FROM THE STARTRestarts from the beginning, with the kit and furniture full again
BACKLeaves with no reward

Leaving the memory midway (closing the screen) also means leaving with no reward.

End, record and reward

  • When you finish, the memory's ending screen appears with the kill count.
  • Each memory's record is the highest number of kills ("Record: N kills" on the sheet). Beating the record is highlighted on the ending screen.
  • Finishing unlocks the memory's outfit set straight into the vault wardrobe (see Sewing and clothes). The reward comes the first time; playing again only counts toward the record.
  • Memory sets never drop from loot or zombies (except the Police and Military uniforms, which the police officer zombie and the soldier zombie can also have).

The crow's memory

The crow is the last one. It appears on the shelf on its own once the other six have been finished (each with a recorded record). Finishing it gives your crow a ribbon from each memory on its leg, which shows on its drawing on the map.
